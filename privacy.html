01

Who We Are

CrabSMP ("we", "us", "our") operates the Minecraft server network at play.crabsmp.net and the website at crabsmp.net. The server is owned and managed by the CrabSMP team, led by Ozorical.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website, join our Minecraft server, or interact with our community, and how we use it.

02

Data We Collect

We collect limited data necessary to operate the server and improve the experience:

When you visit this website

  • IP address: logged temporarily by our web host for security and abuse prevention.
  • Browser & device info: standard web server logs (user agent, page requests, referrer). Not linked to your identity.
  • Voluntary form data: if you submit a contact form or join a mailing list, we store your email address.

When you play on CrabSMP

  • Minecraft username & XUID: your player name and unique player identifier (XUID), required to identify your account and save game progress.
  • IP address: we do not have access to your IP address. Connections to the game server are handled through Verixen (dashboard.verixen.com); any collection or processing of IP addresses is governed by Verixen’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • In-game activity: chat logs, inventory, economy transactions, and playtime. Used to prevent cheating and resolve disputes.
  • Stored game data: to operate the server we save the following in our database: your Enderchest contents, Inventory contents, Ranks, Player name, and XUID.
  • Purchase records: if you buy a rank or token bundle, the transaction reference and the rank granted are stored. Payment card details are never handled by us (see Third-Party Services).

One account is intended for use on a single device. CrabSMP does not support using one account across multiple devices: if you join from a different device than the one you normally play on, you do so at your own risk and may lose your progress, items, or other stored data. You accept full responsibility for any such loss.

When you join our Discord

Discord's own Privacy Policy governs data collected through their platform. We do not export or store Discord member data separately.

03

How We Use Your Data

  • To run and maintain the Minecraft server and website.
  • To enforce our rules and handle moderation actions (bans, mutes, appeals).
  • To deliver purchased items (ranks, tokens) to your account.
  • To send service-related emails (e.g., store updates) if you opted in.
  • To investigate and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • To improve server performance and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data, serve targeted advertisements, or use your data for automated profiling.

04

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share limited data in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: hosting companies and payment processors that help us operate (bound by data processing agreements).
  • Legal obligations: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of CrabSMP and its players.
  • Moderation networks: shared ban lists (Minecraft username / UUID only) with partner networks to protect the community.
05

Cookies & Tracking

This website uses no third-party tracking cookies and no analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics) that profile you across the web.

This website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few small preferences in your browser’s own storage instead: whether you have seen the welcome intro, whether you muted the site’s sound, when you last opened each voting site, and a copy of the live server and Discord figures for two minutes, so moving between pages does not ask again. None of it is personal, and none of it leaves your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

The live Discord figures and member avatars on the homepage load straight from Discord, which may set its own cookies. You can block these in your browser settings.

06

Data Retention

  • Connection & IP logs: handled and retained by our infrastructure provider Verixen, not by us; see Verixen’s own policy via dashboard.verixen.com.
  • In-game data (player name, XUID, inventory, enderchest, ranks, economy): retained for the lifetime of your account. Deleted upon written request (see Your Rights).
  • Purchase records: retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance.
  • Email addresses: retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  • Ban / moderation records: retained indefinitely for community safety.
07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data (subject to our retention obligations).
  • Objection: object to certain uses of your data.
  • Portability: request your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below or open a ticket in our Discord. We will respond within 30 days.

Note: Moderation records (bans, mutes) and purchase records are exempt from erasure requests due to community safety and legal obligations respectively.

08

Children's Privacy

CrabSMP is a Minecraft server. We recognise that many of our players may be under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is described in this policy, and we do not display targeted advertising to any user.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

09

Third-Party Services

CrabSMP uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

Verixen

Server hosting and proxy / anti-cheat. Handles all connections to the game server, including IP addresses, under Verixen’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not have access to player IP addresses.

Discord

Community platform. The homepage shows live figures from Discord’s public server widget, loaded by your browser straight from Discord. Discord’s Privacy Policy governs data collected through their service.

Tebex

Our store platform at store.crabsmp.net. Tebex runs the entire checkout, so payment card data is handled by them and never by us. We only receive a transaction reference and the purchased item details. Tebex’s own privacy policy governs data collected during checkout.

VM6

The VPS management software our server runs on. It is used for provisioning and administering the machine itself, not for handling player data.

PayPal

Used for direct donations and available as a payment method at checkout. Payment card and account data is handled entirely by PayPal. We only receive a transaction reference and the purchased item details.

mcstatus.io and mcsrvstat.us

Public server status services. To show whether the server is online and how many are playing, your browser asks them to check play.crabsmp.net. Like any website, they can see the IP address the request comes from; nothing else about you is sent.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and post an announcement in our Discord server.

Continued use of CrabSMP after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
