Who We Are

CrabSMP ("we", "us", "our") operates the Minecraft Bedrock server at play.crabsmp.net and the website at crabsmp.net. CrabSMP is run by Ozorical, who owns and publishes the server and is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach him at ozorical@icloud.com.

NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT SERVICE. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.

CrabSMP, not Mojang or Microsoft, is responsible for the privacy and protection of the data described here. Mojang and Microsoft have no say in how we handle it and cannot answer for it.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit our website, join our Minecraft server, or use our Discord, and how we use it. Our Terms of Service cover the rules of using CrabSMP.

02

Data We Collect

We collect what we need to run the server, keep it fair, and deliver what you buy or earn:

When you visit this website

  • IP address and request details: Cloudflare hosts the website and sees your IP address, browser, the pages you request and the page you came from, in its logs. We do not link these to your player account.
  • Server status: the live status on the home and status pages comes from a small Cloudflare worker on crabsmp.net, so those requests are covered by the same logs.

When you play on CrabSMP

  • Identity: your Minecraft username, your Xbox user ID (XUID), your client UUID, and when we first and last saw you.
  • Device type: we read which kind of device you play on (Windows, Xbox and so on). It is kept as a tag on your player and shown on your nametag and in the Discord player list.
  • IP address: connections to the game server pass through Verixen (dashboard.verixen.com), which handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. While you are online the server software may be able to see the address you connect from, and a data request export can include it. We do not keep your IP address in our player database. The exception is voting: vote sites pass us an address with each vote, which we do store (see below).
  • Game progress: kills, deaths, level, experience, coins, bounty, blocks broken and placed, playtime, ranks, and the contents of your inventory, armour, off-hand, ender chest and vaults. Also your nickname, bio, faction and faction role, homes, battle pass, quests, settings, mail and auction listings.
  • Chat: in-game chat is relayed to a channel in our Discord with your player name and rank.
  • Block history: a record of the blocks you break, place and use, and the containers you open, with the time, dimension and coordinates. It lets staff look into griefing and roll damage back.
  • Moderation records: bans and mutes, and the actions that automatic moderation and staff take, which are written to a private staff log in Discord.
  • Votes: your player name, the vote site, the day and time, whether the vote was verified, whether you have claimed the reward, and the address the vote site sent with the vote. In the standard voting protocol that address is normally the voter’s IP address. We also keep your vote streak.
  • Purchase records: if you buy a rank or token bundle, the transaction reference and the item granted are stored. The server asks Tebex for your pending purchases using your gamertag. Payment card details are never handled by us (see Third-Party Services).

One account is intended for use on a single device. CrabSMP does not support using one account across multiple devices: if you join from a different device than the one you normally play on, you do so at your own risk and may lose your progress, items, or other stored data. You accept full responsibility for any such loss.

When you use our Discord

Discord’s own Privacy Policy governs data collected through its platform. Our bot also keeps some records of its own in our database:

  • Linked accounts: if you link your Minecraft account, your Discord ID and tag, your player name and when you linked.
  • Invites: the invite codes members create, who joined with which code, when they joined and left, and the invite rewards claimed.
  • Creator submissions: if you send in a video, your Discord ID and tag, your player name, the video link, the decision and who made it.

Our separate AI chat bot, the CrabSMP Bot, reads the channels it can see and keeps its own memory of members who talk to it. This is covered in full under Our Use of AI.

03

How We Use Your Data

Under the UK GDPR we need a lawful basis for each use. Ours are:

  • Running the service (contract): to run the server and website, save your progress, deliver purchased items, and give out vote, invite and creator rewards.
  • Keeping the server safe and fair (legitimate interests): to enforce our rules and Terms of Service, handle bans, mutes and appeals, review block history, prevent cheating, duplication, fraud and abuse, and fix bugs. We have weighed this against your privacy and keep only what staff need.
  • Purchase records (legal obligation): we keep records of purchases for financial and legal compliance.
  • Anything optional (consent): for example linking your Discord account. You can withdraw consent at any time by unlinking or by emailing us.

We do not sell your data, serve targeted advertisements, or profile you for advertising.

04

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share limited data in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: the companies listed under Third-Party Services, which host, deliver, pay for or display parts of CrabSMP.
  • Legal obligations: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of CrabSMP and its players.
  • Moderation networks: shared ban lists (Minecraft username and UUID only) with partner networks to protect the community.

Discord, Cloudflare, Tebex and PayPal may process data outside the UK and the European Economic Area. Their own privacy policies describe the safeguards they use.

05

Cookies & Tracking

This website uses no third-party tracking cookies and no analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics) that profile you across the web.

This website sets no cookies of its own. It keeps a few small items in your browser’s own storage instead:

  • Session storage (gone when you close the tab): a marker that you have already opened the site this visit, and a copy of the live server and Discord figures for two minutes, so moving between pages does not ask again.
  • Local storage (stays until you clear it): whether you have seen the welcome intro, and when you last opened each voting site, so the Vote page can show when each one is ready again.

None of it is personal and none of it leaves your browser. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

Some things load from other companies, which can see your IP address when your browser asks for them: the live Discord figures and member avatars on the home page (Discord), and the photo of Ozorical on the home page (Netlify). Discord may set its own cookies. You can block these in your browser settings.

06

Data Retention

  • Connection & IP logs: handled and retained by our infrastructure providers, Verixen for the game server (see dashboard.verixen.com) and Cloudflare for the website, not by us.
  • In-game and Discord records (identity, progress, inventory, ender chest, vaults, ranks, linked accounts, invites, creator submissions): kept for the lifetime of your account. Deleted on request (see Your Rights).
  • Votes, including the address sent with each vote: we do not delete them automatically, so they are kept for the lifetime of your account. You can ask us to remove them.
  • Block history: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Chat relayed to Discord: kept in Discord until it is deleted, under Discord’s own retention rules.
  • CrabSMP Bot memory: your recent messages to the bot are trimmed as newer ones arrive, and its notes about you stay until you ask it to forget them. Say “forget everything about me” to the bot to wipe both at once (see Our Use of AI). The bot’s view of recent channel messages is held in memory only and is lost when it restarts.
  • Purchase records: retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance.
  • Ban / moderation records: retained indefinitely for community safety.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data (subject to our retention obligations).
  • Objection: object to certain uses of your data.
  • Portability: request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawing consent: where we rely on your consent, take it back at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email ozorical@icloud.com or open a ticket in our Discord (see Contact Us). We may ask you to confirm the account is yours first. Our staff can export everything we hold about a player and erase it, and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would like the chance to put things right first.

Note: Moderation records (bans, mutes) and purchase records are exempt from erasure requests due to community safety and legal obligations respectively.

Children's Privacy

CrabSMP is a Minecraft server, and many of our players are young. You must be old enough to play Minecraft under Mojang’s rules. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (13 in the UK), you may only use CrabSMP with the involvement of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is described in this policy, and we do not display targeted advertising to any user.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us. Email ozorical@icloud.com and we will remove it.

Third-Party Services

CrabSMP uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

Verixen

Server hosting, proxy and anti-cheat. Verixen sits in front of the game server and handles connections to it, under Verixen’s own terms and privacy policy. Any connection and IP logs it keeps are its own.

Discord

Our community platform and bot. In-game chat is relayed to a Discord channel, staff and automatic moderation actions go to a private staff log, and the player list is posted there. The home page also shows live figures from Discord’s public server widget, loaded by your browser straight from Discord. Discord’s Privacy Policy governs data collected through its service.

Tebex

Our store platform at store.crabsmp.net. Tebex runs the entire checkout, so payment card data is handled by them and never by us. We receive a transaction reference and the purchased item details, and the server calls Tebex’s plugin service with your gamertag to collect pending purchases. Tebex’s own privacy policy governs data collected during checkout.

PayPal

Used for direct donations and available as a payment method at checkout. Payment card and account data is handled entirely by PayPal. We only receive a transaction reference and the purchased item details.

Cloudflare

Hosts crabsmp.net and runs the worker that answers the server status requests. Its logs include visitor IP addresses, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

mc-api.io

Draws the player skin images shown in some Discord messages. The server builds the image address with your player name, so mc-api.io and Discord can see it.

DuckDuckGo

The CrabSMP Bot sends it short search queries when it needs outside information. The queries come from our computer, not yours, but they can contain words from your message. See DuckDuckGo’s privacy policy.

Anthropic

Makes Claude, the AI coding assistant we use to help build CrabSMP. It receives the code and documentation we work on with it, under Anthropic’s privacy policy. It is not part of the running website, server or bot.

Vote sites

minecraft.buzz, realmexplorer.xyz and minecraftpocket-servers.com. When you vote they see your IP address and your player name, and each sends us a vote that carries an address, as described above. Each has its own privacy policy.

Netlify

Hosts the photo of Ozorical shown on the home page. Your browser fetches it from Netlify, so Netlify sees your IP address.

VM6

The VPS management software our server runs on. It is used for provisioning and administering the machine itself, not for handling player data.

Our Use of AI

We use AI in two places: to help build CrabSMP, and in a chat bot on our Discord. This website does not run AI on your visit, and the game server does not use an AI model.

Building CrabSMP

We use AI coding assistants, such as Anthropic’s Claude Code, to help write and review the code and documentation for this website, the game server and our Discord bot. The assistants run on our own computers and send the code they are working on to their provider. Our aim is to keep player data out of them: they work on source code and documentation, not on the player database. They are not part of the running website or server and never see you when you visit or play.

The CrabSMP Bot

Our Discord server has an AI chat bot, the CrabSMP Bot. It answers questions about CrabSMP and chats about other things. A language model writes its replies, so they can be wrong. Treat it as a helper, not as staff: it cannot ban, mute or punish anyone, and it decides nothing about your account, purchases or appeals. For those, contact us.

  • Where it runs: the model runs on a computer we operate, using Ollama. Your messages are not sent to an AI company, and we do not use them to train or fine-tune a model.
  • When it replies: when you mention it, in its own help channel, and in direct messages if we have turned that on. In a chat channel we choose, it may also join in unprompted.
  • What it reads: it reads the messages in the channels it can see, from everyone, whether or not they are aimed at it. It keeps a short rolling window of the latest ones in memory only, so it can follow a conversation. That window is never saved to disk and is lost when the bot restarts.
  • What it remembers about you: if you talk to it, it saves your Discord ID, your display name, your recent messages to it and its replies with the times, and short notes about you, such as what you like to be called, your platform, your faction or what you are building. They are kept in a file on that computer. Only the most recent messages are kept, and the notes stay until you ask it to forget them or newer notes push them out.
  • Web search: for questions about the outside world it may send a short search query to DuckDuckGo. The model writes the query from your question, so words from your message can appear in it. It never searches for CrabSMP facts, which come from our own notes.
  • Its log: while it runs, the start of each question and each search appears in a log window on our computer, with the sender’s Discord name. We do not save that log to a file.

Each note the bot saves has to quote something you said, and code filters out private details such as age, location, school, health, family and contact details. That filter is automatic and can miss things, so do not tell the bot anything you would not post in the channel.

You control what it remembers. Tell it “remember that…” or “forget that…” to change a note, or “forget everything about me” to wipe your notes and history straight away. You can also email ozorical@icloud.com to have your entry exported or removed (see Your Rights).
