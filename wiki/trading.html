Trading and bounties

Every way to move coins and items between players.

Sending coins Trade Auction house Buy orders Coinflip Bounties Death tax Duel betting

In short

  • Send coins from the menu: Economy, Transfer Money. Up to 50,000 coins at a time, with a 5% fee.
  • swaps one hotbar stack each. Coins cannot go in a trade.
  • The and buy orders () charge sellers 5% when something sells.
  • A bounty is at least 20 coins and lasts 14 days. A new one replaces the old one with no refund.
  • Killing a player with 50 or more coins takes 2% of their balance, up to 2,500.

Sending coins

There is no pay command. Open your menu item, then Economy, then Transfer Money, pick a player and type an amount.

  • The other player must be online.
  • You pay the full amount and they receive 95% of it. If they owe debt, a quarter of what they receive goes to the debt.
  • A frozen balance cannot send or receive.

Trading items

Use to swap items with another player. Coins cannot go into a trade, and there is no fee.

  1. Send a requestRun and pick an online player. The request ends after 60 seconds if they do nothing.
  2. You offer firstChoose one item stack from your hotbar and how many of it to put in.
  3. They counterThe other player offers one stack from their hotbar in return.
  4. You confirmCheck both offers, then confirm or cancel.

You cannot trade with yourself, or while you are already in a trade. To send coins, use Transfer Money above.

Auction house

Open it with or , or from the bottom row of the shop or the Economy menu. You can sell whole stacks in two ways: Buy It Now, which sells straight away at your price, or Auction, where players bid and the highest bid wins.

Listing is free. When something sells, 5% of the price is taken from you. Your rank decides how many things you can list at once and for how long.

You choose a length of 1, 6, 12, 24, 48, 96, 168 or 336 hours, as long as your rank allows it.

Bidding

  • Your bid leaves your balance at once. If someone outbids you, you get it back.
  • The first bid can match the starting bid. Every bid after that must be at least 1 coin higher.
  • A bid in the last 2 minutes pushes the end back to 2 minutes from then. A message goes out 5 minutes before an auction ends.

Selling

  • You can take a listing back if it has no bids, or end it now to sell to the top bidder.
  • If you are offline when something sells, your coins wait in your mailbox. Winners who are offline get their item there too. Find it under More stuff, then Mailbox.
  • The listing screen shows recent sales of the item, so you can price it.

You cannot list spawners, potions, shulker boxes, bundles, spawn eggs, filled maps, books, player heads, or gear enchanted past Protection IV, Unbreaking III or Sharpness V.

Buy orders

A buy order is a request. You say what you want and what you will pay, and other players fill it from their inventories. Use or .

  • You can ask for any item the shop stocks, except potions, at 1 to 100,000 coins each, and up to 6,400 items per order.
  • The full cost leaves your balance when you post the order and is held until it is filled, cancelled or ends.
  • An order lasts 3 days. If you cancel it or it ends, you get back the coins for what was not filled.
  • Players who fill your order are paid at once, less a 5% fee. The items wait in the order until you collect them.

Coinflip

is a flip against the house, not against another player. It is a fair 50% chance at even money: win and you gain your wager, lose and you lose it. Over time you break even.

  • You need at least 10 coins.
  • You choose your wager with a slider, from 10% of your balance up to all of it.
  • After a short "Flipping..." the result is shown to everyone online.

Bounties

A bounty puts coins on a player's head. Anyone who kills that player collects it. Open More stuff, then Bounties in your menu, or use .

  • The target must be online, and your coins leave your balance as soon as you place it.
  • The bounty is announced in chat.
  • The killer collects it, minus a 10% hunter fee.
  • If nobody collects it in 14 days it disappears, and the coins are not refunded.

A new bounty replaces the old one

If the player already has a bounty, yours replaces it. The coins from the earlier bounty are not refunded to whoever placed it.

Death tax

When you kill another player whose balance is at least 50 coins, you take 2% of their balance, up to 2,500 coins. Another 1% of their balance is destroyed, so the player who dies loses both parts. Deaths in duels do not count.

This is why a big balance makes you a target. The lifesteal side of a kill is in Lifesteal and hearts.

Duel betting

You can bet on a duel with . Your stake is 100 to 25,000 coins and at most 25% of your balance. Back the winner and you get 1.1 times your stake; back the loser and you get 0.9 times. How many bets you can place each day depends on your rank. The rest is in Duels.
