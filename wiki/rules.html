Rules and fair play

What is allowed, what is not, and what happens if you cross the line.

The anarchy way Hearts Cheating Conduct Real life Punishments Chat and AutoMod Alts Reporting

In short

  • CrabSMP is lifesteal anarchy. Outside protected areas, griefing, raiding, stealing and scamming in trades are allowed.
  • Never allowed: hacked clients, duping, X-ray, macros, combat logging, alt farming and real-money trades.
  • Spawn, hubs and arenas are protected. No griefing or trapping there.
  • Punishments go from warning to mute to kick. A ban lasts 7 days at least, and every ban can be appealed in the Discord.
  • Report cheaters with /report. Play from a shared connection? Open a ticket.

The anarchy way

CrabSMP is a lifesteal anarchy world. Outside the safe zones there is no truce and no referee. Everything in this section is allowed, so if it happened to you out there, it was fair play.

RuleWhat it says
1Griefing is allowed. Burn it, blow it up, tear it down.
2Raiding is allowed. Find a base, empty it, leave a crater.
3Stealing is allowed. If it is not claimed or locked away, it is yours.
4Scamming in trades is allowed. Trust is a currency; spend it carefully.
5Lying, betrayal and backstabbing are all fair play.
6Trapping players is allowed anywhere outside protected areas.
7PvP is open everywhere outside safe zones. No warning required.
8Camping and spawn killing are allowed inside PvP zones.
9Factions and alliances are allowed; so is betraying yours.
10Teaming and 10v1s are allowed. Bring friends, or do not.
11Base hunting, tracking and bait chests are part of the game.
12Placing a bounty on anyone, including an ally, is allowed.

Protected areas are the exception

Spawn, the hubs and the arenas are protected. Griefing, building or trapping there is not allowed (rule 31).

Lifesteal and hearts

You start with 10 hearts and can bank up to 30. Fighting for hearts is the point of the server. Manufacturing them is not. How hearts work is in the lifesteal guide.

RuleWhat it says
13Kill a player and you take one of their hearts for yourself.
14Every death costs you a heart; fall damage, lava and mobs included.
15Reach zero hearts and you are eliminated from the world.
16Elimination is not the end; a Soul Bottle can bring anyone back.
17Revive Souls drop on a final death. Whoever picks one up decides.
18Hearts can be withdrawn into items, then traded, gifted, stolen or hidden.
19Farming hearts off alt accounts is a ban, not a strategy.
20Trading kills with anyone to inflate hearts is the same offence.
21Never use a second account to feed yourself hearts, items or bases.
22Killing the same player over and over is allowed; just do not arrange it.
23Do not exploit revives or soul drops to duplicate hearts.

Hard rules: cheating and exploits

Anarchy stops where the game itself stops. These rules break the server for everyone, and breaking them gets you removed by staff. Hacked clients and duping get you banned.

RuleWhat it says
24No hacked clients: killaura, fly, reach, speed or anything like them.
25No X-ray, or resource packs that reveal ores, caves, chests or players.
26No auto-clickers, macros or scripts for combat, farming or fishing.
27No duping items, coins or hearts. A dupe damages everyone's economy.
28No abusing bugs or glitches; report them instead.
29No lag machines, crash attempts or deliberate server lag.
30No combat logging. You stay tagged for 15 seconds after a hit.
31Never grief, build or trap inside protected areas: spawn, hubs and arenas.
32No using claims, homes or teleports to escape a fight you started.
33No abusing shop, auction or crate bugs to print coins.
34No ban or mute evasion. Alts used to evade extend the original ban.
35No modifying, proxying or tampering with your connection to the server.
36Locator maps cannot be used. They show player positions and are removed the moment you get one.

These rules keep the server playable and the chat readable. They are not about combat.

38No advertising other servers, Discords, realms or links.
39English only in global chat so staff can moderate it.

Real-life rules

These carry zero tolerance and have no debate. Whatever happens in the world, none of it follows anyone home.

RuleWhat it says
45No real-life threats of any kind. This is an instant permanent ban.
46No doxxing or sharing anyone's personal information.
47No hate speech, racism or discrimination.
48No harassment that follows a player outside the game.
49No NSFW or illegal content in names, skins, builds, signs or chat.
50No jokes about or encouragement of self-harm. Ever.
51No real-money trades. The store is the only place money changes hands.
52No buying, selling, sharing or trading accounts.

Real money

Rule 51 covers any trade of real money for in-game things between players, and rule 52 covers accounts. The store is the only place money changes hands.

Punishments and appeals

Staff hand out punishments themselves, and each one is recorded. From mildest to strongest you can expect a warning, a mute, a kick or a ban. Bans are handled separately from the rest.

WarningStaff tell you what you did wrong and ask you to stop.
MuteBlocks your chat for a set time. You see who muted you, how long is left and the reason when you try to talk.
KickRemoves you from the server. You can rejoin.
BanMinimum 7 days, permanent for serious or repeat offences. You can appeal.
RuleWhat it says
53The minimum ban length is 7 days. There are no shorter bans.
54Serious or repeated offences go straight to permanent.
55Every ban can be appealed in the Discord.
56Appeal honestly; a denied appeal can be reopened, a dishonest one cannot.
57Do not evade a ban while appealing it. Evading ends the appeal.
58Staff have the final decision on every punishment.
59Arguing a punishment in public chat will never change it.
60Staff are not required to share the evidence behind a ban.
61Punishment history follows the account, not the username.
62Bans do not refund purchases, ranks, hearts or items.

Staff can also restrict an account, which blocks chat, factions, auction listings, coin transfers and trades.

Appealing a ban

Join the Discord and open a support ticket. The steps are in Getting help.

Chat filters and AutoMod

Chat is checked before it is sent. You will see a message that your chat was refused, or be muted, if you hit one of these.

  • Messages that are mostly capitals are turned into lower case.
  • Sending the same or a very similar message again within 10 seconds is refused.
  • The same message sent five times by anyone within 5 seconds is refused.
  • There is a 1 second wait between messages.
  • A word list blocks slurs, hate, self-harm, sexual content, profanity and advertising. Advertising covers other servers, other Discord invites and outside links. crabsmp.net and the CrabSMP Discord are fine.
  • Signs are checked with the same list. A bad sign side is cleared.

Strikes

AutoMod counts strikes. Profanity, advertising and spam are 1 strike each, and severe language is 2. Strikes reset after 24 hours with no new one. At 3 strikes you are muted automatically.

Automatic muteLength
First10 minutes
Second30 minutes
Third1 hour
Fourth3 hours
Fifth and after6 hours

The count resets after 7 days without an automatic mute. Profanity and severe language also fine you 2% of your balance. If you have no coins, you are lent 10 coins as debt instead, and debt grows 5% a day up to 10,000. Staff are not checked by AutoMod.

Do not try to get round it

Spacing words out or using lookalike characters to get past the filter breaks rule 41.

Alts and shared Wi-Fi

Alts become a problem when you use them to feed a main account hearts, items or bases (rules 19 to 21), or to get round a ban or mute (rule 34). Using an alt to evade a ban extends the original ban.

If two or more people play from the same Wi-Fi, for example in a family, a dorm or a school, open a ticket in the Discord so staff know you are different people.

Not sure

If you are unsure whether your setup counts, ask in a ticket.
