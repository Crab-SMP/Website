Fishing

Seven custom rods, four loot tables and a pond at spawn.

How custom fishing works The seven rods Where rods come from Loot tables Legendary catches Skill XP and battle pass points

In short

  • Custom loot only works in the spawn pond and only with one of the seven named rods.
  • 25% of casts reel in nothing. The Coral Rod cuts that to 10%.
  • Rods cost 40,000 to 950,000 coins in the shop, or you can win them from crates and the battle pass.
  • Better rods use better loot tables: Tide, Gilded, Nexus and Crab King. The best prizes, such as Crab and Nexus gear, are rare catches.
  • Every catch gives 15 Fishing XP, or 100 for a legendary one.

How custom fishing works

The seven custom rods only give their loot in the spawn pond, which is any water inside the spawn area. Fish anywhere else and the rod behaves like a normal enchanted fishing rod. A plain vanilla rod in the pond also fishes normally and gets no custom loot.

Your catch shows up in chat. Legendary catches are announced to everyone at spawn and in the Discord.

  • Every cast has a 25% chance to reel in nothing. An empty reel gives no loot, no perk, no skill XP and no points. The Coral Rod cuts this to 10%, and the Fishing skill perk Steady Line cuts it by a fifth more: 20%, or 8% with the Coral Rod.
  • Rods lose durability like any tool. Unbreaking helps, and the Nexus, Abyssal and Crab King's rods also have Mending.
  • You can rename a rod at an anvil without losing its perk.
  • Fishing keys arrive as key items in your inventory.

The seven rods

Each rod uses one of four loot tables. Coral, Prismarine and Abyssal are side steps: they use the same table as the rod before them but swap the perk.

Loot tables

Chances are the share of catches that are not empty. Multiply by 0.75 to get the chance per cast, or by 0.9 with the Coral Rod. Amounts are shown before the name. Coin catches pay up to 40% more with the Fishing skill perk Haggler.

The Nexus and Crab King tables also have rare catches. Every catch first has a small chance to be a rare one: 0.15% on the Nexus and Abyssal rods and 0.6% with the Crab King's Rod. A rare catch is then one of the rare prizes, picked by weight. The Fishing skill perk Lucky Lure makes rare catches 25% more likely.

Legendary catches

Legendary catches are the big ones: they are announced to everyone at spawn. The Tide and Gilded tables have none. The Nexus table has one and the Crab King table has six. All of them are rare catches except the Crab King's top coin catch and the Netherite Block.

Skill XP and battle pass points

Every catch that is not empty gives Fishing skill XP: 15 for a normal catch and 100 for a legendary one. Battle pass points come from the points entries on the loot tables, so a lucky catch can push your pass forward.

The Fishing skill's Double Hook reaches a 15% chance at level 30 to hook an extra reward from the same table, and Triple Hook at level 30 makes that two extra rewards. Its other perks cut empty reels, raise coin catches and make rare catches more likely.
