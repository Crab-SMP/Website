Combat and safe zones

Where you can fight, where you cannot, and what a fight does to you.

Safe zones and graces Where PvP is on Combat tag Combat logging After a kill Item rules Ender pearls Factions

In short

  • Spawn is safe. Almost everywhere else is PvP, including the wild and Freeplay.
  • Hitting a player tags you both for 20 seconds. You cannot teleport or open the menu while tagged.
  • Logging out while tagged drops some of your items.
  • A player kill in the overworld refills your health. Every player kill moves coins and drops the loser's head.
  • Combat is plain Bedrock combat, except that wind charges launch you higher. Faction members cannot hurt each other.

Safe zones and graces

You cannot be hurt in the places below. Graces are short windows where you cannot take damage.

Where PvP is on

PvP is on everywhere that is not listed above. That includes the wild, Freeplay outside its safe circle, the King of the Hill area and the PvP Arena. Bring only gear you can afford to lose.

Hitting a player inside spawn opens their profile, with buttons to ask for a duel or start a trade. To fight someone properly, see duels.

Combat tag

When you hit another player, with a weapon or a projectile, you are both tagged. Every hit refreshes the timer. You see "You are now in combat" when it starts and "You are no longer in combat" when it ends.

It does not apply inside spawn, in the AFK zone or in a duel.

Length
20 seconds
Refreshed
On every hit

What it blocks

  • Every teleport: homes, spawn, warps, the wild, TPA, /back and faction homes. A countdown already running is stopped.
  • Opening the menu item.
  • Joining the ranked duel queue.
  • Riding on players or being stacked on them.

Combat logging

Leaving the game while tagged drops part of your inventory where you stood.

RuleValue
Items dropped35% of your droppable stacks
Minimum1 stack
Maximum6 stacks
Hearts lostNone

Armour, items that are kept on death and locked slots are safe. The server announces who combat logged, and the dropped items are removed from your inventory when you rejoin.

Do not log out in a fight

Anyone nearby can pick up what drops, and staff are alerted. Wait for the tag to end first.

After a kill

Siphon

When you kill a player in the overworld, your health is refilled to full a moment later, and you see "Siphon restored your health". It works anywhere in the overworld, but not in duels, the Nether or the End.

Death tax

When a player kills another player and the victim has at least 50 coins, the killer takes 2% of the victim's balance, up to 2,500 coins. A further 1% is burned and goes to nobody.

Player heads

Every death outside a duel drops that player's head. Its description shows the time, the place and who or what killed them.

Hearts

Deaths cost a heart and kills win one. See Lifesteal and hearts.

Bounties

You can put coins on a player's head, and whoever kills them claims it. See bounties.

Item rules

  • Bundles and locator maps are removed from the server.
  • Wind charges are capped at 128 carried at once. Extras are taken.
  • End crystals, respawn anchors and maces are allowed.
  • Explosions do not break blocks in the PvP Arena or the duel arena.

Wind charges

When your wind charge bursts on a block within about 2 blocks of you, it launches you further than it does in normal Bedrock.

  • Throw one at your feet while looking steeply down and you are launched straight up.
  • Throw one while looking ahead and you dash forward and a little up. The faster you are already moving, the further the dash goes, so you can chain them.
  • Other players within about 2 blocks of the burst are pushed away from it and lifted.

Apart from Siphon and wind charges, combat is plain Bedrock combat, with normal Bedrock attacks and knockback.

Ender pearls in arenas

In the PvP Arena and the duel arena, a pearl thrown at a wall within 3 blocks is cancelled. If a pearl would land you inside blocks or outside the arena, you are sent back to where you threw it. In an active duel, pearls are switched off.

Factions

Members of the same faction cannot hurt each other, ever.

Cheaters

Hacked clients and duping get you banned. If you see someone cheating, use /report or tell staff in the Discord.
