Duels

Fight one on one with no risk. Your items and hearts are safe, and you can bet on or rank up in duels.

Starting Arena and queue Match flow What you keep Kits Betting Ranked Shards and custom kits

In short

  • Run /duel, or punch a player at spawn and press Ask to Duel.
  • You keep everything. No items, hearts or coins are lost, and you go back to where you were.
  • There is one arena. If it is busy you queue and can spectate.
  • Spectators can bet with /bet, from 100 to 25,000 coins.
  • Ranked duels need 10 placement matches and pay prizes by rank each season.
  • Win duels to earn Duel Shards and spend them on custom kits.

Starting a duel

A duel is a fair fight against one player. You keep everything you own, so there is nothing to lose. You can challenge someone from a command, from the menu, or by punching them at spawn.

  • Players can switch off duel requests in settings.

The arena, queue and spectating

There is one duel arena, so only one duel can run at a time. If it is busy, you and your opponent join a queue and carry on playing where you are. You are told your place in the queue.

  • While you wait you are offered the chance to spectate the current match from the stands.
  • Using while spectating stops spectating but keeps your queue place. Using it a second time gives up your place. Using or also stops spectating.
  • Fighters have to stay inside the arena. If you step out, you are teleported back to where you started.
  • Blocks in the arena cannot be broken or placed.

How a match works

Every round starts with a short intro and a 3 second countdown where you cannot move. Then the chat says FIGHT!.

  • You lose a round when your health would drop to half a heart. You do not really die.
  • Both of you are healed between rounds.
  • Both fighters have 20 hearts for the fight, whatever your real hearts are. You get your real hearts back afterwards. See lifesteal for hearts.
  • Only your opponent can hurt you.
  • Totems of Undying are removed for the fight, ender pearls are disabled and your cosmetics are hidden.
  • Commands are blocked except and .
  • The result is announced in chat, like winner defeated loser (2-1) in a duel!. Wins and losses are added to your stats.

The tip line at the top of the screen shows your clicks per second and combo, your opponent's, and the round time. You can hide it with Disable CrabUI in settings.

What you keep

Nothing is lost in a duel. Your inventory and armour are saved before the fight and put back exactly as they were afterwards, and you return to where you were before it started. That includes items you used up or damaged in a duel with Own Gear.

  • No hearts are lost and no one gains a heart.
  • No death tax, no bounty and no head drop apply.
  • The combat tag does not apply in a duel.
  • Leaving the server, using or dying counts as a forfeit, and a forfeit is a loss. If the server restarts mid duel, your items are restored when you next join and you are sent to spawn.

The Combo kit

Combo has zero knockback. Each hit only lifts the target a little, so you can chain hits together.

Betting on duels

Spectators can bet on a casual duel with . You bet on one fighter while betting is open: before a round starts, and again between rounds. You cannot bet on ranked duels or on your own duel, and you get one bet per duel.

  • Back the winner and you get 1.1 times your stake back, a 10% gain.
  • Back the loser and you get 0.9 times your stake back, a 10% loss.
  • The winning fighter is paid 25% of the winnings of the people who backed them, and the losing fighter is paid 10% of the losses of their backers. It is paid on top, so it does not come out of your bet.
  • If a duel never finishes, for example both players leave or the server restarts, bets are refunded.

Ranked duels

Ranked duels give you a rank that is reset every season, with prizes for climbing. Open them with , or Duels, then Ranked Duels. The menu has Find Match or Leave Queue, Prizes and a top 10 Leaderboard.

  • Rating: everyone starts each season on 1000. It is a hidden number that goes up when you win and down when you lose. A win is worth at least 15, and your rating never goes below 0.
  • Placements: your first 10 matches only tell you "Placement match N/10 done". After the tenth you are told your starting rank.
  • Matches: best of 3, with both players on the same kit, picked at random from Iron, Diamond, Netherite, NoDebuff, Combo and Axe & Shield. Custom kits are never used.
  • Matchmaking: you can keep playing while you wait. You are skipped while you are in the air, combat tagged or busy with a duel. The rating gap allowed starts at 100 and widens by 10 for every second you wait. The same two players can play at most 4 ranked matches each server day.
  • Leaving, forfeiting or disconnecting counts as a loss. There is no betting on ranked.
  • Your rank shows on and on the punch profile as Unranked (N/10 placed) or the rank and rating.

Ranks and prizes

Each prize is paid once per season. Reaching a rank pays every rank up to it that you have not been paid for, so placing straight into Gold pays Copper, Iron and Gold. Dropping and climbing back pays nothing again. Prizes are claimed after a ranked result or when you open the ranked menu. Reaching Crab Champion is announced in chat. When a new season starts, everyone does placements again and can earn the prizes again. Keys are for crates.

Duel Shards and custom kits

Duel Shards are a currency you earn from duels. You spend them on your own kits for friendly duels. Shards cannot be bought with coins. Your balance shows in the Custom Kits menu and shop.

  • You earn 1 shard for winning any duel, casual or ranked, forfeits included. Only 4 wins against the same opponent pay a shard each server day.
  • Ranked ranks pay shards too. See the prize table above.
  • You can save up to 3 kits. Creating one costs 10 shards. Editing is free and deleting refunds nothing.
  • Custom kits are for friendly duels only. Both fighters use the challenger's kit, and the accept form lists what is in it.
