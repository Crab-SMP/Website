Shop and selling

How to buy, how selling pays, and what every item costs.

Opening Rules Sections Enchantments Dealers Selling Price lookup

In short

  • Open the shop with . It sells 1,486 items at fixed prices.
  • Every sale pays 75% of the listed sell price, for every rank. There are no multipliers.
  • sells everything sellable in your inventory at once; does it on a timer.
  • Enchanted books are bought with XP levels, not coins.
  • Search the price lookup at the bottom for any item.

Opening the shop

Type or open your menu item, then Economy, then Shop. You see one tile for each section, plus tiles for Spawners, Enchantments and the Auction House. The bottom row has the Sell Menu, your balance, Search and Close.

Click an item to open its buy screen. You can buy 1, a quarter stack, a full stack, or any amount with a slider, and sell 1, a stack or all of it from the same screen. Use Search to find an item by name.

Shop rules

  • Prices are fixed. They do not rise or fall with how much is bought.
  • You cannot buy more than fits in your free inventory slots.
  • You can carry at most 128 wind charges.
  • A purchase of 25,000 coins or more is announced in chat.
  • Potions are buy only; you cannot sell them back.
  • Plushies are free if you have a Crab rank. Everyone else pays 2,500 coins, or 7,500 coins for the four boss plushies. They cannot be sold. See Collectibles.
  • A frozen balance cannot open the shop. See Frozen balance.

Enchantments shop

The Enchantments tile sells enchanted books, and it charges XP levels instead of coins. A book costs its level times the price per level, and you can buy up to 64 books at once if you have the free slots. Each book takes one slot.

For example, Sharpness V costs 25 levels, Protection IV costs 20, Efficiency V costs 20 and Mending costs 30.

Selling

Every sale pays 75% of the listed sell price, for every rank. Ranks, boosters and events do not change it, and there are no sell wands. The total of a sale is rounded down.

You can sell everything in the shop that has a sell price, plus many more items that the shop does not stock. You cannot sell potions, shulker boxes, bundles, filled maps, milk buckets, plushies, or plain mob spawners.

Sells every sellable stack in your inventory, hotbar included, and lists what sold. It ignores favourites.
Opens a chest of your sellable items. Click an item to switch it off for this sale. Favourite Mode starts your favourite items switched off every time.
Sells your inventory on a timer. Every player can use it.

Auto sell

sells on a timer of 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes. The default is 1 minute, and a sale can land up to 5 seconds late. You choose one of two modes: sell everything except a list, or sell only a list of up to 300 items. Hotbar slots are skipped unless you switch them on, and the sale notice can go to chat, the action bar or nowhere.

It only sells plain stacks, so nothing enchanted, renamed or damaged, and it pauses while you are in a duel, in a trade, in creative or spectator, or frozen.

Frozen balances get nothing

If your balance is frozen, and take your items without paying. Wait for the review to end first. See Frozen balance.

Spawners are sold from Shop, Spawners, Sell Spawners, not with these commands.
