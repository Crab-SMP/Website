The world

How big it is, where you can build, and what the server tidies up for you.

World border Nether and End No land claims No build zones Lag clear Sleeping Mob stacking Spawn is far away

In short

  • The Overworld border is 15,000 blocks from the centre in each direction.
  • Nothing outside spawn and the arenas is protected. There are no land claims, so hide your base and keep valuables in a vault.
  • You cannot build within 1,000 blocks of spawn or the arenas, or within 250 blocks of Freeplay.
  • Every 20 minutes the server clears dropped items, XP orbs and some mobs. Pick up what you need first.
  • Night is skipped when 3 players are asleep.

World border

Each dimension has a square border centred on 0, 0. The numbers below are how far the border is from the centre along each axis.

DimensionBorderSize
Overworld15,000A 30,000 by 30,000 square
Nether1,875The Overworld border divided by 8
The End10,000A 20,000 by 20,000 square
  1. 100 blocks outYou get a warning: "You are approaching the world border!"
  2. 50 blocks outRed particles show where the edge is.
  3. At the borderYou are pushed back 5 blocks inside, and staff are told.

You cannot set a home on or past the border, and neither can a faction. Staff can change the border sizes, so these are the current values.

Nether and End

Both are ordinary survival dimensions with no extra rules beyond the border. You can walk in through a portal, or jump straight in with /wild, which asks which dimension you want.

Tip

The Nether border is much smaller than the Overworld one, so travel there covers less ground. A random Nether teleport lands you 250 to 1,800 blocks out on each axis.

There is a safe hub in the Nether where PvP is off and no mobs spawn. See Spawn map for the other safe places.

No land claims

Nothing out there is protected

CrabSMP has no land claims. Factions do not claim land either. Anyone who finds your base can break into it and take what is inside.

Only spawn, the arenas and the Nether hub are protected. Everywhere else is fair game, so plan for it:

  • Build far from spawn and from the paths other players take. Random teleports scatter people across the whole map.
  • Keep your best items in a vault or your ender chest instead of a chest at home. Without CrabExtra, /echest works at spawn only.
  • Do not carry more than you are happy to lose in a fight. Every death costs a heart. See Lifesteal.
  • Play with a faction. Faction members cannot hurt each other. See Factions.
  • Do not tell strangers where you live. Set a home so you can get back without a map.

Hacked clients and duping get you banned. If you think someone is cheating, use /report.

No build zones

Near some places you cannot break blocks, place blocks, use buckets or light fires. The message tells you how many blocks further out to go.

AroundNo build radius
Spawn1,000 blocks
PvP Arena1,000 blocks
Duel arena1,000 blocks
Freeplay and the KOTH hill250 blocks

You also cannot set a home inside any of these zones. Spawn and the arenas are about 100,000 blocks from the centre of the world, so you will not meet these zones while you are out exploring.

Lag clear

Every 20 minutes the server clears out clutter so it stays smooth. You get a warning at 30, 15 and 5 seconds.

It removes, in all three dimensions:

  • Dropped items, including dropped Heart items
  • XP orbs, arrows and tridents
  • Hostile mobs that have no name tag
  • Some farm animals: bats, chickens, cows, mooshrooms, pigs, polar bears and sheep

Do not leave loot on the ground

Anything dropped is gone at the next clear. Pick up your items when you see the warning. Farm animals can be cleared too, so do not rely on a large herd standing around.

Sleeping

You do not need everyone in bed. Night is skipped as soon as 3 players in the Overworld are asleep. If fewer than 3 players are online, everyone online has to sleep.

Mob stacking

When the server is busy, mobs of the same type crowding one chunk are merged into stacks of up to 20. Killing a stack drops the items of every mob in it, but only one mob's worth of XP.

Spawn is far away

Spawn is about 100,000 blocks from the centre of the world, well past the border, so you cannot walk back to it from your base. Use /spawn or the MENU item, then Warps. If you walk to within 400 blocks of the centre of the Overworld, you are sent to spawn.

Because you cannot return on foot, set a home before you go to spawn. Rejoining the server also puts you at spawn by default, and the /wild menu is the quickest way back out.
