Crab and Nexus gear

The two families of custom tools and armour, and how to get them.

Two families Abilities What the 3x3 and tree felling break Stats Where gear comes from Knockback Stick

In short

  • Crab gear comes from Epic crates; Nexus gear comes from Mythic crates and does more.
  • Pickaxes and shovels break a 3x3 area, axes fell the whole tree, swords strike lightning on every 5th hit.
  • Nexus tools also give Haste II while held.
  • Extra blocks are never broken in spawn, the PvP and duel arenas, no build zones or the Nether hub, and chests, spawners and similar blocks are always safe.
  • You can also get gear from fishing and the Hunter dealer. All of it mends itself slowly.
  • The Knockback Stick comes from Common crates.

Two families

Custom gear comes in two families. Crab gear is the red one and Nexus gear is the purple one. Nexus does everything Crab does and adds more. Each family is a full set of tools and armour: sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots.

Crab gear Epic crate tier. Area digging, tree felling, lightning and knockback armour.
Nexus gear Mythic crate tier. Everything Crab has, plus Haste II, more damage and tougher armour.

None of it is sold for real money. Every piece lists its ability in its lore.

The armour abilities only work when you wear the full set of four pieces. The knockback only triggers when a player hits you; mobs never set it off.

Nexus tools give Haste II while you hold them. Haste from the Mining or Woodcutting skill does not stack with it: you get whichever is higher. See Skills.

What the 3x3 and tree felling break

The pickaxe and shovel break a flat 3x3 square facing the way you look, and only blocks that tool is right for. The axe fells the whole tree, up to 64 extra blocks of the same kind. Each extra block costs 1 durability.

Never broken by the area or tree felling

  • Bedrock and barriers
  • Command blocks, structure blocks and end portal frames
  • Chests, trapped chests, ender chests and barrels
  • Hoppers, dispensers and droppers
  • Furnaces, blast furnaces and smokers
  • Brewing stands, beacons and spawners
  • Crafting tables, enchanting tables, anvils, lecterns and bells

Where the extra blocks are refused

  • Spawn
  • The PvP arena and the duel arena
  • No build zones
  • The Nether hub

In those places you still break the block you hit, as normal.

Skill XP

Only the block you actually hit counts for skill XP and skill perks. The extra blocks from the 3x3 or tree felling give none.

Lightning never strikes inside spawn. It works on mobs and players, and the hit count is kept for each player.

Where gear comes from

SourceWhat you can get
Epic crateEvery piece of Crab gear
Mythic crateEvery piece of Nexus gear
FishingRare catches only. Nexus Rod and Abyssal Rod: Nexus Sword and Nexus Pickaxe. Crab King's Rod: Crab Sword, Pickaxe, Chestplate and Helmet, plus Nexus Sword and Chestplate
Hunter dealerCrab Sword for 25,000 coins, Nexus Sword for 50,000 coins, Nexus Helmet for 18,000 coins

The dealer shows 3 random trades from its pool, each with 2 in stock, and refreshes every hour. For crate odds see Crates, and for the fishing rods see Fishing.

Knockback Stick

The Knockback Stick is a weapon that launches players 3 to 6 blocks when you hit them. It is a Common crate drop.
