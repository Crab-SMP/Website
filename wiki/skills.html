Skills

Five skills to level up, a perk tree for each, and coins at every level.

Open the menu Levels, XP and rewards Perks How to earn XP Top players Pet crab

In short

  • Five skills: Mining, Woodcutting, Farming, Combat and Fishing. Open them with /skills.
  • The level cap is 30. Each level pays 50 coins times the level, and levels 10, 20 and 30 also give crate keys.
  • Each skill has a root perk that grows every level, plus five tree perks that unlock at levels 5, 10, 15, 25 and 30.
  • Blocks you placed yourself give no XP.
  • Reach level 30 in Mining, Woodcutting and Farming to unlock the pet crab.

Open the menu

Type or press the Skills button in your stats menu. The first screen is your profile: your total level out of 150, how many of the 25 tree perks you have, how close you are to the pet crab, and a row for each skill with its level and XP. It also has a Top Players button.

Click a skill to open its tree. The left side shows your level, your XP, the coins for the next level and the next key reward. The tree shows every perk with its level and name. Click the root at the bottom for the full detail page, with the root perk, every tree perk, the level rewards and the XP sources. Click any other node to see that perk and how much XP you still need for it. All Skills takes you back to the profile.

Every time you earn XP, a small card appears at the top right of the screen with your level and progress. It slides away a few seconds after you stop. Level ups are announced in chat with the coins you were paid and any perk you unlocked. Creative mode earns no XP.

There are five skills. Each one has a root perk that gets better with every level and five tree perks that unlock along the way.

Key rewards

Levels 10, 20 and 30 of every skill also give crate keys, once per skill. With all five skills maxed that is 10 Common keys, 5 Epic keys and 5 Mythic keys.

  • The keys arrive as key items in your inventory. If it is full, they drop at your feet.
  • Skill keys always arrive in full. The 75% chance on earned keys does not apply to them; see Crates.
  • If you were already past one of these levels when key rewards were added, you get those keys the next time you earn skill XP or open .

Perks

Every skill has a root perk that is always on. Its chance grows by the same amount each level, so you feel it from level 1 and it tops out at level 30. On top of that, each skill has five tree perks that unlock at levels 5, 10, 15, 25 and 30 and then stay on for good.

The trees follow one pattern. Level 5 is a handy extra. Levels 10 and 25, on the left branch, are one perk in two ranks. Levels 15 and 30, on the right branch, are about loot, and the level 30 perk usually makes the root perk drop triple instead of double.

How the perks work

  • A bonus drop is rolled again with the tool you used, so Fortune, Silk Touch and Looting count for it too. With Motherlode, Heartwood or Plunder it is rolled twice.
  • Prospector and Timber work on ores and logs only, not on plain stone. Spoils works on hostile mobs, except the Warden, the Wither and the Ender Dragon.
  • Green Thumb works on wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart and cocoa that you plant.
  • Bountiful works on grown wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot, nether wart and cocoa, and on melons and pumpkins you did not place. Sugar cane and cactus never drop double. Farming's double drops come only from Bountiful, not from the root perk.
  • Replant works on wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot and nether wart, not cocoa. It takes one seed from your inventory (wheat seeds, a carrot, a potato, beetroot seeds or nether wart), so keep some with you. The new crop starts from the beginning.
  • Smelter works on iron, gold and copper ore, deepslate versions included, and turns the Prospector bonus into ingots as well.
  • Seedkeeper drops the matching sapling: a mangrove propagule for mangrove logs, and a fungus for crimson and warped stems.
  • Haste from Mining or Woodcutting and Haste from a Nexus tool do not stack. The higher one applies.
  • Night Owl stops when you put the pickaxe away or climb above Y 0. It never turns off Night Vision you switched on with .
  • Farmland Speed wears off a few seconds after you step off. It does not replace a stronger Speed effect, such as one from a potion.
  • Brute, Second Wind and Wisdom only count mobs that give Combat XP, so Brute never adds damage against players.
  • The fishing perks only work on spawn pond catches with a named rod. Haggler raises coin catches, not the Gilded Rod's bonus coins. See Fishing.

How to earn XP

Only the block you hit or the mob you kill counts. Extra blocks broken by the area and tree tools of Crab and Nexus gear give no XP and no Prospector bonus.

Placed blocks

Blocks you placed yourself give no XP. Placing a log, ore, stone, melon, pumpkin, sugar cane or cactus and breaking it again earns nothing.

Top players

Press Top Players on the profile screen to pick a board. All Skills ranks everyone by total level across the five skills, with total XP breaking ties. Each skill also has its own board, ranked by XP. The Top Players button inside a skill tree opens that skill's board straight away.

  • Each board shows the top ten, then your own rank.
  • Boards refresh every few minutes, so a level you just reached can take up to five minutes to show.

Pet crab

Get Mining, Woodcutting and Farming all to level 30 and you unlock a pet crab. Combat and Fishing are not needed.

Open the menu with to summon or dismiss it and to pick a colour. Changing colour is free and you can do it whenever you like.

Unlock
Mining, Woodcutting and Farming at 30
Colours
Blue, Red, Gold, Green, Purple
  • The crab follows you and cannot be hurt, pushed, renamed or tamed by hand.
  • It leaves when you log off and comes back when you join.
  • If it falls far behind or you change dimension, it reappears beside you.
