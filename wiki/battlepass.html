Battle pass and quests

Earn points, climb 100 tiers and claim coins, keys and Season X cosmetics.

Open the battle pass Tiers and point costs How to earn points Rewards Bonus tiers Get premium Daily quests Claim your rewards

In short

  • Open it with /bp. There are 100 tiers and endless bonus tiers after that.
  • Earn points from daily quests, voting, events, fishing and crates. 39,700 points gets you to tier 100.
  • Everyone gets coins, keys and items. Premium pays more and adds the Season X cosmetics.
  • Get premium for $5.99, with the CrabDemon rank, or free after 3 days of playtime.
  • Daily quests reset at midnight UTC. Claim them in /bp on the Quests tab.
  • Each earned key has a 75% chance to arrive.

Open the battle pass

Type , use the battle pass NPC at spawn, or open it from the Other menu. The screen has two tabs: Pass for the tier list and Quests for your daily quests.

The Pass tab is a scrolling list of tiers. The free reward for each tier is on the left and the premium reward is on the right. Claim All collects every reward you have unlocked on both tracks.

The current season is Season X. Staff start a new season from time to time, with no fixed length. When that happens your points and claims reset. Premium and every cosmetic you earned stay.

Tiers and point costs

There are 100 tiers, and the pass never really ends: after tier 100 you keep earning bonus tiers. Each tier costs a little more than the last, starting at 100 points and rising by 6 every tier.

Why a key did not arrive

Every key you earn has a 75% chance to arrive, and this applies to battle pass keys too. On average three out of every four keys land. It is not a bug, so a missing key will not be added later.

Bonus tiers

After tier 100, a bonus tier arrives every 1,000 points, with no limit. The free track gives 1 Epic key for each bonus tier and the premium track gives 1 Mythic key. Both go through the 75% key chance.

Get premium

Premium is permanent and carries over to every future season. There are three ways to get it.

Buy it Premium Battlepass costs $5.99 in the store.
CrabDemon The CrabDemon rank includes it. CrabPlus and CrabExtra do not.
Play for it It unlocks for free once you reach 3 days of total playtime.

A premium tier you reached before you had premium is not lost. Claim it once premium is yours.

Daily quests

There are 13 daily quests, and all of them are active for everyone at once. Each one pays battle pass points. At level 1 all 13 together are worth 680 points a day (850 with premium).

Quests reset at midnight UTC every day. Progress and claims reset, but quest levels do not.

Quest levels

Every time you claim a quest it goes up a level for good. The next level asks for 1.5 times as many (rounded up) and pays double the points. Levels never reset, not daily and not between seasons. Each quest can be claimed once a day.

Claim your rewards

  1. Finish the questChat tells you when a quest is complete.
  2. Open the battle passType and open the Quests tab.
  3. Claim itClick the ready quest, or press Claim All. Points land in your pass straight away.

Tier rewards are claimed on the Pass tab, with the same Claim All button.
