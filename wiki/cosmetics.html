Cosmetics

Wings, halos, capes, pets, voids and emotes, and how to get them.

Opening the wardrobe The five slots How many you get by rank Colourways Season X battle pass set Cosmetic bundles Emotes Other rank cosmetics

In short

  • Open the wardrobe with /cosmetics or the mannequin at spawn.
  • Five slots: wings, headwear, cape, pets and voids. Wings and capes share your back, so wear one or the other.
  • 113 cosmetics: CrabPlus gets 18, CrabExtra 60 and CrabDemon 109, and the battlepass adds 4 more.
  • Colourway bundles cost $0.79, or $1.29 for animated ones, and are yours for good.
  • Any Crab rank gets 38 emotes and custom join, leave and kill messages.

Opening the wardrobe

  1. Open itType , open the Donator hub, or hit the cosmetic mannequin at spawn.
  2. Pick a tabThere is one tab for each slot, with a live preview of your own character.
  3. EquipClick a tile to wear it. Click the worn tile again to take it off, or press Unequip All to clear everything.

Cosmetics are hidden while you are in a duel and come back afterwards. If a rank ends, the cosmetics it unlocked are taken off you.

The five slots

WingsBack piece.
HeadwearHalos and the Crab Crown.
CapeBack piece.
PetsWyrms and guardians that follow you.
VoidsA ring of dark tumbling cubes at your feet.

Wings or cape

Wings and capes both sit on your back, so you cannot wear them together. Equipping one takes the other off.

How many you get by rank

There are 113 cosmetics. Each rank includes everything from the ranks below it, so CrabPlus sees 18, CrabExtra sees 60 and CrabDemon sees all 109 rank cosmetics. The other 4 come only from the battlepass. See Ranks.

Colourways

Most cosmetics come in colourways. A colourway has a matching wings, halo, cape, wyrm, guardian and void. There are 17.

Flat colours (13)
Tide, Verdant, Ivory, Storm, Ember, Gilded, Blush, Frost, Crimson, Royal, Abyss, Onyx and Jet
Animated (4)
Rainbow, Inferno, Aurora and Nebula
One-offs
Demon Wings, Crab Crown, CrabSMP Cape, Sunset Cape, Royalty Cape, Reef Guardians and Bone Guardians

Season X battle pass set

Four red and gold cosmetics come from the premium battlepass. Reach the tier and claim it. No rank unlocks them, and you keep them after the season ends. A locked one shows a battlepass badge in the wardrobe, and clicking it tells you which tier you need.

Cosmetic bundles

You can buy a colourway as a bundle on the store: its wings, halo, cape, wyrm and void. Guardians are not included; they stay a CrabDemon perk.

Flat colours
13 bundles at $0.79 each
Animated
4 bundles at $1.29 each (Rainbow, Inferno, Aurora, Nebula)
Not sold
Season X and the one-offs

Bundles are permanent and survive a rank ending. If you own a bundle but no rank, you can still open the wardrobe and wear what you own. The in-game store hub has a Cosmetics tile listing every bundle and price.

Emotes

Any Crab rank can use emotes. Type or use the Emotes NPC to pick one. All 38 are below.

Griddy, Dab, Floss, Cute Dance, Buggie Dance, Rat Dance, Hakari Dance, Doodle Dance, Penguin Dance, Kazoch Kick, Head Spin, Piglin Celebration, JoJo Pose, Golden Freddy, Viltrumite, Salute, Tilt, Laugh, Sad, Bored, Look Down, Shake Head, Snow Angel, Sleep In Air, Praise The Sun, Bow, Proper Bow, Prostration, Prayer, Beckon, Wave, Shrug, Joy, Hurrah, Point Up, Point Forward, Point Down, Well What Is It.

Griddy can be done while moving; sneak to stop it. The others stop when you move.
