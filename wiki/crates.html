Crates and keys

The three crates, how to get keys, and what you can win.

The crates Opening Getting keys Common prizes Epic prizes Mythic prizes Goodie bags

In short

  • There are three crates: Common, Epic and Mythic. Click one at spawn or run .
  • Each key can win nothing: 25% on Common, 18% on Epic, 10% on Mythic.
  • Press Claim to get your prize. Closing the menu does not lose it.
  • Keys come from playing, voting, , kits, dealers and more. Earned keys arrive 75% of the time.
  • Use Key Conversion in to turn physical keys into digital ones.

The three crates

There are three crates, and each one needs its own key. The better the crate, the better the prizes.

Every key can come up empty

Each key has a flat chance of winning nothing, and the key is still used. The chances above are on top of that, and the prize tables below already include it.

Opening a crate

  1. Get a keyYou need a key for that crate. The next section lists every way to get one.
  2. Find a crateClick a physical crate at spawn, or run to open one from anywhere. The odds are the same either way.
  3. Choose how manyOpen 1, 3 or 5 keys at once. One key plays the spinning reel; 3 or 5 show a results chest.
  4. Press ClaimThe prize is only given when you press Claim. If you close the menu it reopens, and if you disconnect during a spin it is waiting for you when you join again.
  • There is a 5 second wait between opens.
  • Only one prize can wait to be claimed at a time, and only one player can open the same physical crate at once.
  • The rarest prizes play a special sound and are announced at spawn.
  • Hitting or clicking a crate without a key opens nothing.

Getting keys

Keys are held either as items or as a digital balance. When you open a crate, digital keys are used first.

Only 75% of earned keys arrive

Every key you earn from playing, voting, the daily reward and the Battlepass has a 75% chance to arrive. Two vote keys count as 1.5, so you get 1 key half the time and 2 keys the other half. The server keyall is not affected, and neither are keys from skills, kits, dealers or crates.

Key conversion

Open and pick Key Conversion to turn all your physical keys for one crate, or for every crate, into digital keys. Keys never turn into coins.

Goodie bags

Use a goodie bag to open it. Each bag gives several rolls, and every roll picks one reward on its own, so the same reward can come up twice. Bags come from crates and the dealers.

BagRollsCoinsKeys
Common goodie bag22,000 to 8,000 coins, about 10% per roll1 Common key, about 5% per roll
Rare goodie bag310,000 to 30,000 coins, about 12% per roll2 Common keys, about 5%; 1 Epic key, about 5%
Mythic goodie bag415,000 to 40,000 coins, about 8% per roll2 Epic keys, about 8%; 1 Mythic key, about 7%

The rest of each bag is ores and blocks, food, XP bottles, and enchanted gear: iron from the Common bag, diamond from the Rare bag and netherite from the Mythic bag. The Mythic bag can also give enchanted books, an elytra, a beacon, a Nether Star, a totem, a mace and a lifesteal heart.
