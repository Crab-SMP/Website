Spawners

Buy a spawner, leave it near you, and collect the drops.

Buying and selling How they work Stacking Placing and breaking Locks Champions Income Bonus drops Friendly prices Enemy prices

In short

  • Buy spawners in , under Spawners: from 30,000 to 500,000 coins.
  • They only work with a player within 16 blocks, or further with faction upgrades, and they store the drops for you.
  • You can place 25 within 100 blocks, or 50 with a faction upgrade. Breaking one needs Silk Touch.
  • Selling one back pays 40% of the shop price.
  • Stand within 10 blocks and a spawner may send out a Champion mob to kill for bonus loot.
  • Income is capped so a spawner takes at least about 30 active hours to repay its price.

Buying and selling

Open and pick the Spawners tile. It has Friendly Spawners (37 mobs), Enemy Spawners (34 mobs) and Sell Spawners. You can buy up to 64 spawners at a time, and prices run from 30,000 coins for a cow to 500,000 coins for a warden.

Sell Spawners pays 40% of the current shop price for every spawner in the item, and a stacked spawner item counts each one. The XP stored inside is not paid for. and the auction house do not take spawners.

You can also win spawners as rare crate prizes. See Crates and keys.

How they work

Place a spawner like a block. No mobs appear. Every 50 seconds the spawner works out what a group of mobs would drop, adds any bonus drops, and stores the items and XP inside it, so there is nothing to fight. The only mob that ever appears is a Champion.

  • It only works while a player is within 16 blocks. When nobody is near, it waits. Faction range upgrades raise this to 28, 40 or 52 blocks; see the faction shop.
  • Click it to see its stack size, how much it stores and its XP. From there you can collect XP, open the storage, remove the stack, turn the floating text on or off, or change its lock.
  • Storage is 45 slots for every spawner in the stack, and XP is up to 2,500 for each. When either is full, it stops adding.
  • The Sell All button in the storage pays a little less than taking the items out and using , as a fee for the convenience.

Stacking

Use a spawner item of the same mob on one you have placed to add it to the stack. A bigger stack makes more per cycle, with less extra for each one you add. The biggest stack depends on the mob:

Max stackMobs
96Most animals and fish, and the cheaper enemies such as zombie, skeleton, spider, slime and creeper
64The mid tier enemies, such as enderman, blaze, ghast and the wither skeleton
32Guardian, piglin brute, breeze and shulker

Placing and breaking

  • You can place at most 25 spawners within 100 blocks. If your faction has bought the Spawner Cap upgrade in the faction shop, you can place 50.
  • To break one you need a tool with Silk Touch. You get back one item that carries the whole stack.
  • Stored items go into a chest directly above the spawner if there is one. Otherwise they drop on the ground.
  • A locked spawner can only be broken by the players its lock allows, and explosions never destroy a spawner.

Locks

The lock button in a spawner's menu sets who can open it, stack onto it and break it. Each press moves to the next setting, and the menu shows which one is on.

SettingWho can use the spawner
UnlockedAnyone.
Locked to youOnly you.
Locked to your factionYou and the members of your faction. This setting is skipped if you are not in one.

After the faction setting it goes back to unlocked. Only the player who locked a spawner can change its lock. Anyone else who tries to use it is told it is locked by its owner, or locked to another faction.

Champions

If you stand within 10 blocks of a spawner while it makes its drops, it may send out a Champion: a real, tougher copy of its mob that appears on top of the spawner, named in gold. Everyone within 30 blocks sees this in chat:

» A Champion Zombie crawled out of a spawner! Kill it within 2 minutes for bonus loot.
  • Kill it within 2 minutes and it drops up to four cycles' worth of the spawner's loot on the ground, and you get a cycle's worth of XP straight away. After 2 minutes it disappears.
  • A Champion has extra health and Resistance I, and a hostile one fights back.
  • The chance is about 3% each cycle for a single spawner and grows with the stack, up to 20%. The spawner's menu shows its current chance.
  • A spawner only has one Champion at a time and waits 10 minutes before the next. It needs an empty block on top.
  • If the spawner is locked against you, its Champion drops nothing when you kill it.

Creeper, ghast, phantom, guardian, elder guardian and warden spawners never send out Champions, and neither do the water mobs: cod, salmon, pufferfish, tropical fish, squid, glow squid, dolphin and axolotl.

How much they earn

A spawner cannot pay itself back quickly. What it produces is capped, so that selling everything it makes will never repay its price in less than about 30 hours of someone standing near it. Dearer spawners take longer: about 34 hours from 100,000 coins, 38 from 200,000 and 42 from 350,000.

Stacking helps, but with diminishing returns, so a stack of 10 earns less than ten times one spawner. The "ceiling per hour" in the tables below is the most a spawner's sellable drops can be worth in an hour when you are near, at prices. It is a ceiling, not a promise: a mob whose drops are worth little earns less, and a spawner with nobody near earns nothing.
