Spawners

Buy a spawner, leave it near you, and collect the drops.

Buying and selling How they work Stacking Placing and breaking Income Friendly prices Enemy prices

In short

  • Buy spawners in , under Spawners: from 300,000 to 5,000,000 coins.
  • They only work with a player within 16 blocks, and they store the drops for you.
  • You can place 25 within 100 blocks of each other. Breaking one needs Silk Touch.
  • Selling one back pays 40% of the shop price.
  • Income is capped so a spawner takes at least about 30 active hours to repay its price.

Buying and selling

Open and pick the Spawners tile. It has Friendly Spawners (37 mobs), Enemy Spawners (32 mobs) and Sell Spawners. You can buy up to 64 spawners at a time, and prices run from 300,000 coins for a cow to 5,000,000 coins for a warden.

Sell Spawners pays 40% of the current shop price for every spawner in the item, and a stacked spawner item counts each one. The XP stored inside is not paid for. and the auction house do not take spawners.

You can also win spawners as rare crate prizes. See Crates and keys.

How they work

Place a spawner like a block. No mobs appear. Every 50 seconds the spawner works out what a group of mobs would drop and stores the items and XP inside it, so there is nothing to fight.

  • It only works while a player is within 16 blocks. When nobody is near, it waits.
  • Click it to see its stack size, how much it stores and its XP. From there you can collect XP, open the storage, remove the stack, turn the floating text on or off, or lock it so only you can use or break it.
  • Storage is 45 slots for every spawner in the stack, and XP is up to 2,500 for each. When either is full, it stops adding.
  • The Sell All button in the storage pays a little less than taking the items out and using , as a fee for the convenience.

Stacking

Use a spawner item of the same mob on one you have placed to add it to the stack. A bigger stack makes more per cycle, with less extra for each one you add. The biggest stack depends on the mob:

Max stackMobs
96Most animals and fish, and the cheaper enemies such as zombie, skeleton, spider, slime and creeper
64The mid tier enemies, such as enderman, blaze, ghast and the wither skeleton
32Guardian, piglin brute and breeze
24Elder guardian, ravager and warden

The tables below give the max stack for every mob.

Placing and breaking

  • You can place at most 25 spawners within 100 blocks of each other.
  • To break one you need a tool with Silk Touch. You get back one item that carries the whole stack.
  • Stored items go into a chest directly above the spawner if there is one. Otherwise they drop on the ground.
  • A locked spawner can only be broken by its owner, and explosions never destroy a spawner.

How much they earn

A spawner cannot pay itself back quickly. What it produces is capped, so that selling everything it makes will never repay its price in less than about 30 hours of someone standing near it. Dearer spawners take longer: about 34 hours from 1,000,000 coins, 38 from 2,000,000 and 42 from 3,500,000.

Stacking helps, but with diminishing returns, so a stack of 10 earns less than ten times one spawner. The "ceiling per hour" in the tables below is the most a spawner's sellable drops can be worth in an hour when you are near, at prices. It is a ceiling, not a promise: a mob whose drops are worth little earns less, and a spawner with nobody near earns nothing.
