Kits

What each kit holds, who can use it and how often.

Overview Starter kit Discord Kit Store kits

In short

  • You get the starter kit on first join. Lost it? Use or the chest at spawn, every 10 minutes.
  • Link your Discord to claim the Discord Kit, once: diamond gear, keys and 5,000 coins.
  • Knight, Warrior and Crusader are sold at store.crabsmp.net. Prices are on the store.
  • There are no rank kits and no kit command.

All the kits

There are five kits. Two are free and three are sold in the store. Ranks do not come with a kit you can claim again and again, and there is no kit command.

Starter kit

You get this when you first join. If you lose your gear, you can get the items again from the starter chest at spawn or with , once every 10 minutes. The 3 Common keys you got on your first join are not given again.

  • Chainmail armour with Protection I and Unbreaking II
  • Iron sword with Sharpness I and Unbreaking II
  • Iron pickaxe, axe and shovel with Efficiency II and Unbreaking II
  • 16 bread
Reclaim
or the starter chest at spawn
Cooldown
10 minutes
First join only
3 Common keys

Discord Kit

Link your Discord account to your Minecraft account to unlock this kit. Open your menu item and pick Discord Kit. You can claim it once per player. Linking is explained in Discord.

  • Full diamond armour with Protection II and Unbreaking III
  • Diamond sword with Sharpness II and Unbreaking III
  • Diamond pickaxe with Efficiency III, Fortune I and Unbreaking III
  • Diamond axe and shovel with Efficiency III and Unbreaking III
  • 16 bread, 2 golden apples and an ender chest
  • 2 Epic keys and 1 Mythic key
  • 5,000 coins

Store kits

Knight, Warrior and Crusader are sold at store.crabsmp.net (opens in a new tab). Prices are on the store. After you buy one, it is delivered by the game.

  • If you are offline, or in a duel, the kit waits and arrives about 3 seconds after you next spawn.
  • Items that do not fit in your inventory drop at your feet, so make room first.
  • A purchase is announced in chat, and keys arrive as digital keys.

Knight

  • Iron armour with Protection II
  • Diamond sword with Sharpness II
  • Iron pickaxe with Efficiency III
  • 16 cooked beef and 3 golden apples
  • 3 Common keys
  • 150,000 coins

Warrior

  • Diamond armour with Protection III and Unbreaking III
  • Diamond sword with Sharpness III, Fire Aspect I, Looting II and Unbreaking III
  • Mace with Density III, and a trident with Impaling III and Loyalty III
  • Diamond axe and pickaxe with Fortune II
  • Shield, a bow with Power IV and 64 arrows
  • 8 ender pearls, 32 cooked beef, 16 golden carrots and 8 golden apples
  • 20 hearts
  • 5 Common keys and 2 Epic keys
  • 500,000 coins

Crusader

  • Netherite armour with Protection IV, Unbreaking III and Mending
  • Netherite sword with Sharpness V, Fire Aspect II, Looting III, Knockback II and Mending
  • Mace with Density V and Wind Burst III, and a trident with Impaling V, Loyalty III and Channeling
  • Netherite axe and pickaxe with Fortune III
  • Shield, a crossbow with Quick Charge III and Piercing IV, and 64 arrows
  • 16 ender pearls, 64 cooked beef, 32 golden carrots and 16 enchanted golden apples
  • 4 totems and 30 hearts
  • 8 Common keys, 4 Epic keys and 2 Mythic keys
  • 1,500,000 coins
