Getting help

Who to ask, and how, for each kind of problem.

Restart first Reports Tickets Appeals Purchases Data requests Staff levels

In short

  • Close and reopen Minecraft first. It fixes a lot.
  • Report a player with /report and a bug with /bugreport.
  • Appeals, purchase problems and data requests go through a ticket in the Discord.
  • Data requests are answered within 30 days.
  • Staff will never DM you first for your password or items.

Restart the game first

Many problems clear up after you fully close Minecraft and open it again. Do this first if a menu will not open, a screen is stuck, items look wrong or you cannot connect. If the problem is still there, use the section below that fits.

Reporting a player or a bug

Report a player

Type and fill in the form. Staff see it in a private channel. Reasons you can pick are cheating or hacking, griefing, harassment or toxicity, inappropriate chat or name, advertising and other.

Remember that griefing outside protected areas is allowed. Read the rules before you report. False reports are punished.

Player
Their exact name
What happened

Evidence

Type and fill in the form. Reporting a bug is better than using it, which breaks the rules.

Include the details

Say who, where and when. A clip or screenshot makes it much quicker for staff to act.

Tickets in Discord

For anything staff need to look at, open a ticket in the Discord (opens in a new tab). A ticket is a private conversation with staff. Use it for appeals, store problems, data requests, alts on a shared connection and anything that does not fit a report.

You can also open the Support page in the in-game main menu, under More stuff. It has short help pages for homes, factions, economy, the mailbox and Discord.

There is no CrabSMP /help command

Typing /help shows Minecraft's own list of commands. For the server's commands, read the commands guide.

Appeals

Every ban can be appealed. Open a support ticket in the Discord and tell staff what happened.

  • Bans last at least 7 days. Serious or repeat offences are permanent.
  • Be honest. A denied appeal can be reopened, but a dishonest one cannot.
  • Do not evade the ban while you appeal. Evading ends the appeal.
  • Staff have the final decision and do not have to share the evidence.
  • Arguing in public chat will not change a punishment.
  • Bans do not refund purchases, ranks, hearts or items.

The full rules are in Rules and fair play.

Purchase problems

Purchases are delivered by the server about once a minute while you are online, and on your next join if you were offline. If yours has not arrived:

  1. Rejoin and wait a few minutesDelivery also runs when you join.
  2. Check your gamertagIt must match the name on your order exactly.
  3. Open a ticket in the DiscordInclude your Tebex transaction id and your gamertag.
  4. Email if you need toWrite to ozorical@icloud.com with the same details.

Refunds and billing are handled by Tebex. More on buying and delivery is in Ranks and the store.

Data requests

You can ask staff what data CrabSMP holds about you. There is no command for it. Open a support ticket in the Discord and say you want a data request. You will get an answer within 30 days.

If you cannot use Discord, email ozorical@icloud.com. Moderation and purchase records are kept even if you ask for your data to be erased. The privacy policy and terms explain more.

Who is who

Staff have different levels. Each level can do everything the ones below it can.

LevelWhat they handle
Helper and StaffMute players, freeze and spectate, teleport to players, view inventories, look up block history and broadcast messages.
AdminRestrict accounts, view ender chests, run bounties and events, and look after server maintenance.
Sr AdminRoll back and restore blocks, and fix factions, homes and auctions.
LeadMoney, donator plans, battle pass and leaderboards.
OwnerRanks, cosmetics, keys, and data resets.

Every staff action is logged. See staff tags to recognise them in chat. Staff will never message you first to ask for your password or your items.
