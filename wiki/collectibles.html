Plushies, heads and the podium

Things to collect and show off: mob plushies, player heads and the top three at spawn.

Plushies Every plushie Player heads The podium

In short

  • There are 91 plushies in /shop: 250 coins each, 750 for the four bosses, and free with any Crab rank.
  • A player who dies drops their head, with when, where and how they died in the lore.
  • Heads and the podium show real skins, but only classic 64x64 or 128x128 skins.
  • The podium at spawn ranks the top three players by a score out of 100.
  • Coins count most, then duel wins, then kills and playtime. It updates every 15 minutes.

Plushies

Plushies are decorative blocks shaped like mobs. Place one, then use it and it squeaks with a soft cloth sound plus that mob's own sound.

There are 91. Buy them from in the Plushies category. They do not drop from crates, mobs or fishing, and you cannot sell them back.

Player heads

When a player dies, from any cause and not only a PvP kill, they drop their own head. Deaths inside a duel do not drop one.

The head is named after the player and its lore shows:

  • The player's name
  • When they died, in server time
  • Where they died, as coordinates
  • Who killed them, or the cause of death, such as Fell to Their Death, Blown Up by a Mob or Struck by Lightning

Place the head and it shows the owner's real skin. The item in your hotbar stays a Steve head. Breaking a placed head gives the same named head back with its lore, and explosions do too. Placed heads are visible within 40 blocks.

Skins

Only classic skins show: 64x32, 64x64 and 128x128. Character Creator skins, which are usually 256x256, show as Steve.

The podium

Three figures at spawn show the best, second best and third best players overall, wearing their real skins. Each name tag shows the place, the name and the score, for example Score 47.3. An empty place reads Nobody yet.

Hit a figure to see that player's stats and the points they earn from each one.

How the score works

The score is out of 100. For each stat you earn its weight multiplied by your value, divided by the best value on the server. The best player in a stat earns that stat's full weight.

Every player counts, online or not. The podium updates every 15 minutes.

Skins

Only 64x64 and 128x128 skins are drawn. Others show as Steve, and a changed skin shows after a restart.
