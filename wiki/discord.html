In short

  • Join at discord.gg/es6snXqXW5.
  • Link with /link in Discord, then enter the 6 character code in the in-game Discord menu within 10 minutes.

Joining the Discord

The CrabSMP Discord is where tickets, announcements and the chat bridge live. Join with this link (opens in a new tab), which goes to the invite discord.gg/es6snXqXW5.

You do not need to link an account to join, to read, or to talk to the game through the chat bridge.

Linking your account

Linking connects your Discord account to your Minecraft account. Each Discord account links to one Minecraft account.

  1. Join the DiscordUse the invite above.
  2. Run /linkType in Discord. The bot replies privately with a 6 character code. It expires after 10 minutes.
  3. Open the Discord menu in gameType , open it from the main menu, or press Link Discord on the welcome screen. Then press Link Account.
  4. Enter the codeType the code and press Link Account again.
  5. DoneThe game says your account is linked, announces it in chat and gives you the Discord Kit.

If linking fails

MessageWhat to do
Invalid or expired code.Check the code, or run /link again for a new one.
Code expired.Run /link in Discord to get a fresh code.
This Discord is already linked.That Discord account is already tied to a Minecraft account. Unlink it first.
This Minecraft account is already linked.Your game account already has a Discord linked. Unlink it first to use a different one.

To unlink, press Unlink in the in-game Discord menu or run in Discord. The kit only ever pays once, even if you link again.

The chat bridge

In-game chat and a Discord channel are joined together. What you type in game appears in Discord, and what is typed there appears in game.

[ Discord] Name » hello from Discord
  • Your in-game messages show in Discord under your name, with your skin as the picture. Only your first tag shows, such as [Member].
  • Discord users do not need to be linked. They show under their Discord display name.
  • A reply shows who it replies to, and mentions show in yellow.
  • A picture posted in Discord is not shown in game. The message shows with [Image attachment] after its text.
  • A name that contains the word discord or clyde cannot be relayed, because Discord refuses those names.
  • Discord messages are checked for scams.

Leaderboards

The Discord has a leaderboard channel with seven boards. Each one is a picture of the top 10 players, with the top three on a podium and every player's face from their skin. They update every 10 minutes.

  • Top CrabSMP Players: the overall score out of 100, the same ranking as the podium at spawn.
  • Top Money, Top Kills, Top Duel Wins, Top Miners, Top Playtime and Top Voters.
