Commands

Every player command, grouped by what you want to do. Click one to copy it.

Getting around Lifesteal Money and rewards Social and PvP Settings and info Rank perks

In short

  • The MENU item or /menu does most things without a command.
  • Square brackets mean the argument is optional; angle brackets mean you must give one.
  • Teleporting to a player is in the menu, not a /tpa command.
  • /back needs CrabExtra or CrabDemon; /nick needs CrabDemon.
  • To report a cheater, use /report.

Getting around

Opens the main menu, the same as the MENU item.
Teleports you to spawn. Also works as /hub.
Opens the random teleport menu: Overworld, Nether or The End. Without a rank there is a 2 minute wait between uses. Also works as /rtp.
Opens the warps menu. Also works as /warpmenu.
[name] Teleports you to that home. On its own it opens the homes menu.
Opens the homes menu.
[name] Sets a home where you stand. On its own it opens the homes menu. Names cannot contain spaces.
[name] Deletes a home. On its own it opens the homes menu.
Takes you to where you last died, or to where you last used a home, spawn or faction home teleport. Needs CrabExtra or CrabDemon. The point is forgotten when you log out.
Turns flight on or off. Works only inside spawn and needs any Crab rank.

Teleporting to a player

There is no /tpa command. Open the menu, choose Teleport, then Send, View or Cancel. See Teleports and homes.

Lifesteal

CommandWhat it does
[amount] Turns hearts into Heart items you can give or sell. The default is 1. You always keep at least 1 heart.
