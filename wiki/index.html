Quick answers

The questions staff hear most.

I died to fall damage and lost a heart. Is that a bug?

No. Every death outside a duel costs one heart, whatever killed you: falling, lava, mobs or players. Duels never cost hearts.

Lifesteal and hearts

I'm stuck in spectator at spawn. What now?

You ran out of hearts. Another player can bring you back with a Soul Bottle, or a revive can be bought on the store. You come back on 10 hearts.

How revives work

Why can't I teleport or open the menu?

You are probably combat tagged. Hitting or being hit by a player tags you for 20 seconds, and teleports and the menu are blocked until it ends. Moving during the 3 second countdown also cancels a teleport.

Combat and safe zones

Where is /tpa?

There is no /tpa command. Open the menu, pick Teleport, then Send and choose the player. They accept from the same screen.

Teleporting to a player

Is my base protected?

No. There are no land claims, so anything outside spawn and the arenas can be found, raided or broken. Hide your base well and keep valuables in your vault.

The world

Why can't I build or set a home here?

You are too close to spawn, the PvP Arena or the duel arena (1,000 blocks), or to Freeplay (250 blocks). The message tells you how far out to go.

Homes

My items vanished from the ground.

Every 20 minutes the server clears dropped items to keep it fast. Chat warns you 30, 15 and 5 seconds before.

Lag clear

Do I lose my items in a duel?

No. Your inventory is saved before the fight and given back exactly as it was. Duels cost no hearts and no coins.
