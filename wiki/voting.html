Voting

Three sites, a reward on each, and bonuses for voting every day.

Vote sites Rewards Daily reset Streaks How to vote

In short

  • Vote on 3 sites once each per day. Each vote pays 1,000,000 coins plus 3% of your balance, battle pass points and keys.
  • The day resets at midnight UTC, not 24 hours after your last vote.
  • Streaks of 3, 7, 14 and 30 days pay extra coins and keys.
  • Your first vote gives you the permanent Voter rank.
  • Offline when you voted? It is paid on your next join.

Where to vote

There are three vote sites. You can vote on each one once a day, and every vote pays the same reward.

What a vote pays

Each vote gives you all of this.

RewardAmount
Coins1,000,000 coins
Balance bonus3% of your balance, up to 500,000 coins
Battle pass points250 to 500, picked at random
Common keysUp to 2. The second is not always given.
Voter rankOn your first vote, permanent

Your vote is also announced in chat. Vote on all three sites in one day and you get the reward three times. The balance bonus grows with your coins until it reaches the cap.

When votes reset

A voting day is a calendar day in UTC, not 24 hours from your last vote. It starts at midnight UTC. After midnight you can vote on every site again, even if you voted a few hours earlier. A second vote on the same site in the same UTC day does not count.

Vote once a UTC day

Voting at least once in each UTC day keeps your streak alive. You do not have to vote at the same time every day.

Vote streaks

Vote on at least one site on consecutive UTC days to build a streak. Miss a whole day and the streak goes back to 0. When you reach a streak tier you get its bonus on top of the normal reward.

StreakBonus coinsBonus keys
3 days60,000 coins1 Epic key
7 days200,000 coins1 Mythic key
14 days400,000 coins2 Epic keys
30 days1,000,000 coins2 Mythic keys and 3 Epic keys

The 30 day bonus repeats every 30 days after that, at 60 days, 90 days and so on. Bonus keys are the most you can get and are not always all given.

How to vote

  1. Open the vote menuType in game, or go to the vote page.
  2. Pick a siteOpen one of the three vote sites from the menu or the table above.
  3. Enter your nameType your exact Bedrock gamertag when the site asks, and press Vote.
  4. Get paidThe reward arrives in game, and chat announces your vote. Your first vote also gives you the Voter rank.
  5. Repeat on the other sitesEach site counts on its own, so vote on all three for the full reward.

If you vote while you are offline, the reward is paid when you next join. In the Discord you can also run to collect it, and to see the sites, which ones you voted on today, your streak and your total votes.

Reward missing

Check the gamertag you typed, then rejoin. If it still does not arrive, open a ticket in the Discord. See Getting help.

Vote didn't pay?

Rejoin first. If it still has not arrived, ask in the Discord.
