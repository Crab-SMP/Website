Getting started

Join on any device, claim your kit and make the most of your first ten minutes.

Joining Your first join New player protection Starter kit The MENU item Your first ten minutes Where next

In short

  • Join at play.crabsmp.net, port 19132. It is free on every device.
  • You start with a starter kit and 5 minutes of protection from all damage.
  • Your kit comes back from the starter chest or /starterkit every 10 minutes.
  • The MENU item opens everything. It is given back every time you join or respawn.
  • Link your Discord for a free Discord Kit with diamond gear.

Joining

CrabSMP runs on Minecraft Bedrock, so phones, consoles and PCs can all play together.

PC and mobile

  1. Open the server listIn Minecraft Bedrock, go to Servers, then Add Server.
  2. Enter the addressServer IP is play.crabsmp.net.
  3. Enter the portPort is 19132, which is already the default.

Joined from a friend invite?

On the welcome screen press Add Friend and Play, then add the server on the profile that opens. If you skip this, you will not be able to join back.

Your first join

  1. The screen fades inYour first moments are a short fade from black.
  2. You land somewhereStaff choose where new players start: a random spot in the wild, spawn, or a mix of both. In the wild you see "You started out in the wild at X, Y, Z." If the land will not load, you start at spawn and can use /rtp when you are ready.
  3. You get your kitThe starter kit goes straight into your inventory, along with 3 Common crate keys.
  4. The welcome screen opensIt has four buttons: Add Friend and Play, Read Tutorial, Terms of Service and Link Discord. Link Discord lets you link your account and claim the free Discord Kit, then brings you back. The screen stays open, even after you rejoin, until you press Add Friend and Play.
  5. You get the Discord linkAfter you press Add Friend and Play you see the Discord invite and a reminder of the free Discord Kit. If you started in the wild, you are also reminded that /spawn takes you to spawn.
  6. Tips startShort tips appear in chat as you play. Turn them off with /tips off, or see them all again with /tips reset.

You can replay the welcome screen any time with .

When you rejoin later you are put back at spawn by default. If you rejoin in the Overworld away from spawn, you get 15 seconds where nothing can hurt you.

New player protection

If you start in the wild, you cannot take damage for your first 5 minutes. It counts real time, so it carries on through relogs and restarts. You can still hit other players during it. You are told when it runs out.

Spawn is always safe: no damage at all within 400 blocks. See Spawn map.

Getting it back

Right click the starter chest at spawn, or run . You can do this once every 10 minutes, and your first join starts that timer too. A reclaim gives the gear again but not the 3 keys.

The MENU item

Your first ten minutes

  1. Check your gearYour starter kit is in your inventory. Equip the armour and keep the bread for later.
  2. Head into the wildType to teleport to a random spot. Without a rank you wait 2 minutes between uses.
  3. Set a homeUse where you plan to settle, so you can always get back. See Teleports and homes.
  4. Link your DiscordJoin the Discord, link your account in game, and claim the Discord Kit once: coins, full diamond armour and tools, golden apples, and Epic and Mythic keys. See Discord.
  5. Claim your daily rewardType every day to build a streak.
  6. VoteType for the vote sites and their rewards. See Voting.

Every death costs a heart

You start with 10 hearts and lose 1 each time you die, including from falls and lava. At 0 you are out until someone revives you. Read Lifesteal before you go exploring.
