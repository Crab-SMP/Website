Coins and earning

Where coins come from, easiest first, and how to keep track of them.

In short

  • You start with 500 coins. Check your balance with and the richest players with .
  • Stand in the AFK zone at spawn for steady coins, and vote on all 3 sites every day.
  • Claim every day and run at least every 2 hours.
  • There is no pay command. Send coins from the menu: Economy, Transfer Money.
  • Swearing costs coins and can put you in debt; 25% of what you earn then goes to paying it off.

Coins and XP levels

Coins are the server's money. You earn them, spend them in the shop and send them to other players. Your balance is on the sidebar, and every amount is a whole number of coins.

XP levels are a second price. The enchantments shop and the two dealers charge levels instead of coins for some things, so keep some of your levels for them. There is no exchange between levels and coins.

You start with 500 coins the first time you join. Crate keys are a third kind of currency, covered in Crates and keys.

The AFK zone

The AFK zone is a round area at spawn. Stand inside it and you are paid every 10 seconds, with no input needed. A message in chat tells you your rate, what the next 30 minutes will pay and how much of your daily cap is left.

  • A new player earns 30 coins every 10 seconds. The rate rises with your total playtime and reaches 100 coins every 10 seconds at 4 hours.
  • Every time you walk in, a bonus of 12% of your balance (up to 500,000) is paid out over the next 30 minutes, on top of the base pay.
  • The longer you stay in one visit, the less it pays: 70% after 75 minutes and 40% after 150 minutes. Step out and back in to start again.

Keys can miss

Earned keys only arrive 75% of the time. If a daily key misses, you get 500 coins instead.

Playtime rewards

Just by playing you earn keys automatically: a Common key every 60 minutes, an Epic key every 150 minutes and a Mythic key every 480 minutes. Each one has a 75% chance to arrive, and chat says "KEYALL" when it does.

When you reach 3 days of total playtime, the Premium Battlepass unlocks for free. See Battlepass.

Cashout

Use to turn your playtime into coins. The game banks the time you have played since your last cashout, up to 120 minutes, and pays 7% of your balance for a full bank, up to 500,000 coins. A half full bank pays 3.5%.

  • You need at least 10 minutes banked.
  • Cashing out empties the bank.
  • Time past 120 minutes is lost, so cash out at least every 2 hours.
  • From 30 minutes banked, chat reminds you now and then.

Voting

Voting is one of the best earners in the game. Each vote on one of the three sites pays 1,000,000 coins, 3% of your balance (up to 500,000 coins), 2 Common keys and some Battlepass points, and you can vote on every site once a day. Votes made while you are offline are paid when you next join. The streak bonuses and the links are on the voting guide.

Sending coins

There is no pay command. Open your menu item, then Economy, then Transfer Money, pick an online player and type an amount. You can send up to 50,000 coins at a time, and the receiver gets 95% of it. The limit, the fee and the other ways to move coins are in Trading and bounties.

Debt

The only way into debt is the chat filter. Swearing or using a severe word costs 2% of your balance (at least 1 coin) on top of the usual warning. If you have no coins, the bank lends you 10 coins, which becomes debt.

  • Debt grows by 5% a day, even while you are offline, and stops growing at 10,000 coins.
  • Until it is cleared, 25% of every coin you earn goes to the debt automatically.
  • You are told about your debt when you join.

To pay it off sooner, use : pay everything you can, or choose an amount. If you think the filter fined you by mistake, ask staff in the Discord.

Frozen balance

Staff can freeze a balance while they review it. Nothing is taken from you and you can keep playing, but no coins can move in or out. You see "Your balance has been frozen for review" with a case number, and the shop, selling, transfers, coinflip, the auction house, buy orders, AFK pay and auto sell all stop working until the case is closed.

Do not sell while frozen

and take your items, but a frozen balance cannot be paid, so you get nothing for them. Wait for the review to finish before you sell anything.

Staff lift the freeze themselves, so there is nothing for you to do except wait. If you want to know how long it will take, ask in the Discord and quote your case number.
