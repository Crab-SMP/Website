Factions

Team up with up to 17 other players, share a bank and home, and fight wars for coins.

Creating Command Roles Bank Home Shop Land and friendly fire Wars

In short

  • Creating a faction costs 10,000 coins. Use /faction to open the menu.
  • A faction holds 8 members, or up to 18 with bought slots.
  • The shared bank holds up to 50,000,000 coins. Co-leaders can withdraw 3 times an hour in total.
  • No land is claimed by factions and nothing outside spawn is protected. Faction mates cannot hurt each other.
  • Wars last 15 minutes, are started from the War button, and the most kills takes both wagers.

Creating a faction

A faction is a team of up to 18 players with a shared bank, a shared home and a way to go to war with other factions. Run to open the faction menu, or create one straight from chat.

  1. Have the coinsCreating a faction costs 10,000 coins, taken from your own balance.
  2. Pick a nameNames are 1 to 15 characters. You can rename later for 25,000 coins.
  3. Run the command

You can only be in one faction at a time. The server holds up to 500 factions. Each faction keeps a log of its last 100 events, which you can read from the Logs button in the menu.

Roles and members

Everyone in a faction has a role. The leader sets roles from Manage, then Members. A faction starts with 8 member slots and can grow to 18 by buying slots in the faction shop.

RoleWhat they can do
MemberPut coins in the bank, use the faction home, buy from the faction shop.
AdminEverything a member can, plus invite players.
Co-leaderEverything an admin can, plus withdraw from the bank, promote and demote, kick, rename the faction, start or answer a war and open Manage.
LeaderEverything a co-leader can, plus set, move or remove the home and disband the faction.
  • The leader cannot be kicked or demoted.
  • If the leader leaves, the first remaining member becomes the leader. If the last member leaves, the faction is deleted.
  • Invites do not expire. They stay until they are accepted, rejected, or the faction is full or gone.

The faction bank

Every member can pay coins into the shared bank. Only co-leaders and the leader can take coins out.

Bank cap
50,000,000 coins. A deposit that would go over the cap is trimmed and the rest stays with you.
Withdrawals
3 per hour for the whole faction, not per player.
Shop and wars
Buying from the faction shop and putting coins on a war do not count as withdrawals.
Disbanding
The bank is split evenly between all members and sent to each of them by mail.

The faction home

A faction has one shared home. The leader sets it, moves it or removes it. Any member can then teleport there with .

  • It cannot be set in a no build zone, or on or past the world border.
  • It uses the normal teleport countdown. Moving cancels it, being in combat stops it, and you cannot use it during a duel.

The faction shop

The Shop button in the faction menu sells two things, both paid from the faction bank. Any member can buy them.

UpgradeCostWhat you get
Extra member slot30,000 coins for the first, then 15,000 coins more each time. The tenth slot costs 165,000 coins.One more member, up to 10 extra slots.
Bank Interest I100,000 coins1% a day on the bank.
Bank Interest II250,000 coins. Needs Interest I first.2% a day on the bank.

Interest is paid once every 24 hours on up to 500,000 coins of the bank balance, and never takes the bank over its cap. That makes the most you can earn 5,000 coins a day with Interest I and 10,000 coins a day with Interest II.

Land, protection and friendly fire

Factions do not claim land. There are no claims, no claim slots and no protected chests. Nothing outside spawn is protected, so build and store things with that in mind.

Members of the same faction can never hurt each other. Friendly fire is always off and there is no setting for it. Your faction mates can still be hurt by anyone else.

Faction wars

A war is a timed fight between two factions with coins on the line. Whoever gets the most kills wins both wagers. Wars are started from the War button in the faction menu; there is no command for it. Only co-leaders and the leader can start or answer one.

  1. Pick a rivalChoose a faction that is not already at war and has a co-leader or leader online.
  2. Set the wagerChoose an amount from 10,000 coins to 5,000,000 coins. Both faction banks must hold that much.
  3. Wait for an answerThe challenge lasts 2 minutes. An officer of the other faction can accept it in that time.
  4. The war startsBoth wagers leave the banks and are held until the end. The war runs for 15 minutes, anywhere in the world. It is announced in chat, with a warning at 1 minute left.
  5. Count the killsOnly kills between members of the two warring factions count. Duel deaths do not.
OutcomeWhat happens
One faction has more killsThat faction wins both wagers. If that would take its bank over the 50,000,000 coins cap, the extra goes back to the loser.
Tie, including 0 to 0Both wagers are returned.
A faction disbands during the warIt forfeits and the other faction wins.

A faction can only be in one war at a time. Wars carry on through a server restart.
