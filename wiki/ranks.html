Ranks and the store

What each rank gives, how to spot staff, and how a purchase reaches you.

The ranks Compare perks Earned ranks Staff tags Other items Delivery Not arrived

In short

  • Three permanent ranks: CrabPlus $2.99, CrabExtra $5.99 and CrabDemon $8.99.
  • /back starts at CrabExtra. Free players already get /echest in spawn and 15 minutes of night vision.
  • Voter and Creator are earned for free, not bought.
  • Staff tags use a shield and pixel lettering. Staff never DM you first.
  • Purchases arrive within about a minute if you are online, or on your next join. Late? Open a Discord ticket.

The three ranks

Each rank is bought once and is permanent. You hold one at a time, so an upgrade replaces the one you have. Higher ranks include everything the lower ones give. All three are quality of life and cosmetics.

CrabPlus$2.99. /fly in spawn, 11 homes, emotes and 18 cosmetics.
CrabExtra$5.99. Adds /back, /echest anywhere, 5 vaults and 60 cosmetics.
CrabDemon$8.99. Adds 26 homes, 2 extra lives, nicknames and all 109 cosmetics.

Never pay to win

The store sells comfort and looks, and the server promises no pay to win.

Details

  • Free players already have /echest in spawn and 15 minutes of night vision every 2 hours. The ranks add more time, and use anywhere.
  • /back starts at CrabExtra, not CrabPlus. CrabPlus players do not have it.
  • Extra lives (CrabDemon): if a death would eliminate you, one of your 2 extra lives is used instead. You come back at 10 hearts with a few seconds of protection, and chat tells you how many are left.
  • Keep one hotbar item (CrabDemon): you choose a hotbar slot, and that item stays with you when you die.
  • Homes: anyone can buy 4 more slots with coins, at 1,000, 2,000, 3,000 and 4,000 coins.
  • Night vision time starts when you switch it on. Switching it off keeps the time you have left, and the 2 hour wait counts from the first time you turned it on.
  • If you lose a rank, its tag, extra homes, slots, nickname, tagline, nametag effect, flight and cosmetics go with it. Your active auction listings are cancelled and returned by mail.

More on homes, vaults and auctions is in the teleports guide, shop guide and cosmetics guide.

Two ranks cost nothing. They show beside your Member tag, for example [Member] [VOTER].

Staff tags

Staff ranks replace the Member tag. They are drawn with a coloured shield and pixel lettering, not plain text, and nobody can type those characters into a name. If a tag looks like ordinary letters, it is not a staff tag.

Staff never message you first

Staff will not DM you to ask for your password, items or payment. Report anyone who does with /report or a ticket.

How delivery works

Purchases are delivered by the server itself. Nothing needs to be redeemed.

  1. Buy on the storeGo to store.crabsmp.net and enter your Bedrock gamertag exactly.
  2. The server checks every minuteIt checks for new purchases about once a minute.
  3. Online: it arrives at onceYou hear a sound and the rank, item or revive is applied. Ranks also give you the PERKS item and announce the purchase in chat.
  4. Offline: it waitsThe purchase is saved and applied the next time you join. Mail also holds some deliveries.

Payments, billing and refunds go through Tebex, the checkout provider.

If a purchase does not arrive

  1. Wait a few minutes and rejoinDelivery runs when you join as well as every minute.
  2. Check the gamertagIt must match your Bedrock name exactly, with the same spaces.
  3. Open a Discord ticketInclude your Tebex transaction id and your gamertag. Staff can check the queue and fix it.
  4. Email if you cannot use DiscordWrite to ozorical@icloud.com with the same details.

More ways to get help are in Getting help.
