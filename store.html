Questions

Before You Buy

Which username do I enter at checkout?

Your exact Minecraft Bedrock gamertag, spelled and spaced the way it appears in game. It is the only detail the rank is matched against, so a typo means the perks land on nobody.

Do ranks expire or auto-renew?

Neither. Every rank is a one-off payment and permanent. There is no subscription and nothing to cancel.

Can I upgrade to a higher tier later?

Yes. Buying a higher tier replaces the one you have rather than stacking with it, so you always hold exactly one rank and it is the newest one you bought.

How long does delivery take?

Usually a few minutes. If it has been longer than that, join our Discord and open a ticket with your transaction ID and we will sort it.

Who handles payments and refunds?

Tebex Limited. They own and operate the checkout, take the payment, and handle billing support and refunds. Card details never reach CrabSMP — all we receive is a transaction reference and the item you bought.

What happens if I remove my own rank in game?

Removing a plan from the in-game PERKS menu strips everything it gave you — the tag, chat colours, Premium Battlepass, every cosmetic, your nickname, flight, extra homes and vaults. As our terms say, doing it to yourself is not something we can undo.
