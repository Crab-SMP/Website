Features

What We Have

01

Discord Integration

Chat, joins, deaths, and staff actions relay straight to Discord through our own custom bot. Talk to the server from your phone, even when you are not in the game.

02

A World To Conquer

Mine deep caves, push out to a 7,000 block border, and claim the land as your own. The Nether and End are open and waiting.

03

Free Starter Kit

Every new player is handed a one time starter kit the moment they join. Link your Discord account in game to unlock an even stronger bonus kit.

04

A Spawn Built To Last

A hand built spawn with the shop, warps, NPCs, and a PvP arena. The whole zone is grief and PvP protected, so you always start safe.

05

Stunning Visuals

Cherry blossom hills, balloon dotted skies, and detailed builds. CrabSMP looks sharp whether you play on console, mobile, or PC.

06

The Crab Engine

CrabSMP runs on our own TypeScript engine. Factions, lifesteal, the economy, crates, spawners, and anti-cheat are all built in house and updated constantly.
