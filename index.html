Fair Play

Not Pay To Win

Nobody buys their way past you on CrabSMP. Ranks are quality of life and cosmetics, they never touch combat, and the strongest players on the server are the ones who put the hours in. That is the whole point.

Ranks Never Touch Combat

No bought damage, no bought hearts, no stat multipliers. A rank gets you convenience and cosmetics, and that is where it stops.

Everything Is Earnable

Crate keys come from voting, playtime milestones, and daily rewards. Quests grant XP toward 100 RPG levels. You never have to spend a penny to get any of it.

Free From The First Second

No whitelist, no paywall, no trial. Join, take your free starter kit, link Discord for the bonus kit, and go build something.

Your Money Runs The Server

Every penny goes into hosting, upkeep and development. It buys uptime for everyone, not an advantage for one person.

Free to play. Always. The leaderboard is earned, not bought.
