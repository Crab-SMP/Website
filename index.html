Lifesteal

How lifesteal works on CrabSMP

  1. You start with ten hearts.

    Everyone spawns on the same ten. What happens to them next is up to you.

  2. Win a fight. Take one.

    Every kill steals a heart from the player you beat and adds it to your bar.

  3. Lose one, and it’s theirs.

    Every death costs you a heart, and if a player took you down, it goes straight to them.

  4. Lose them all. You’re out.

    Run out of hearts and you are eliminated, locked out of the world.

  5. Unless your team brings you back.

    A teammate spends a Soul Bottle to put you back in the fight, hearts and all.

  6. Climb to thirty.

    Hearts stack to a cap of thirty. Earn them. Then keep them.

Fair play

Not pay to win. Not even close.

Nobody buys their way past you on CrabSMP. Ranks are convenience and cosmetics, and they never touch combat. The strongest players on the server are the ones who put the hours in.

  • No bought power

    No bought damage, no bought hearts, no stat multipliers. A rank stops at convenience and cosmetics.

  • Everything is earnable

    Crate keys come from voting, playtime milestones and daily rewards. You never have to spend a penny.

  • Free from the first second

    No whitelist, no paywall, no trial. Join, take your free starter kit and go build something.

  • Your money runs the server

    Every penny goes into hosting, upkeep and development. It buys uptime for everyone, not an edge for one.

Rules

House rules.

The short version: be decent and play fair. The full version is below.

  1. 01

    Be respectful

    Treat all players and staff with kindness. No toxicity, harassment, or personal attacks.

  2. 02

    No hate speech

    Discrimination, slurs, or offensive language will not be tolerated under any circumstance.

  3. 03

    No griefing

    Destroying or altering others’ builds without permission is strictly prohibited.

  4. 04

    No hacking or exploits

    Cheats, hacks, or third-party software granting unfair advantage are forbidden.

  5. 05

    No AFKing

    Staying AFK for long periods to occupy player slots will result in a kick.

  6. 06

    No duping or glitch abuse

    Exploiting bugs, including item duplication, is not allowed. Report glitches instead.

  7. 07

    No racism or discrimination

    Respect everyone. Racist or offensive behavior will lead to punishment.

  8. 08

    No spamming

    Avoid excessive messages, chat flooding, or bypassing the spam filter.

  9. 09

    Privacy matters

    Do not share personal information or ask others for theirs.

  10. 10

    No real-world trading

    Selling in-game items or currency for real money is prohibited: permanent ban.

Discord vault rules

  • Only one person can enter the vault at a time; otherwise, the kit will not work.
  • Do not share the code outside this Discord. Violating this results in a permanent ban.

King of the Hill rules

  • No teaming: KOTH is a solo event; teaming leads to immediate disqualification.
  • No combat logging: logging out mid-fight in KOTH results in an immediate ban from the event.
  • No outside interference: spectators must not interfere with the ongoing battle.
  1. 01

    Acceptance of terms

    By joining the server, visiting the website or joining the Discord, you agree to the Terms of service. If you do not agree, do not use CrabSMP.

  2. 02

    One account, one device

    Each account is meant for a single device. Joining from a different device is at your own risk and can cost you progress, items or other stored data.

  3. 03

    Staff decisions are final

    Staff can warn, mute, kick or ban for breaking the rules or the Terms, and have the final say in disputes. You can appeal in a Discord ticket.

  4. 04

    Minecraft EULA and Mojang

    You must follow the Minecraft EULA. CrabSMP is not an official Minecraft service and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

  5. 05

    The full documents

    Read the full Terms of service and read the Privacy Policy for purchases, refunds, data and everything else.
